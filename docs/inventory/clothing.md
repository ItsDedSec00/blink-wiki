# Clothing

The Clothing inventory tab covers what you can wear during a session.
Wardrobe is one of the easiest ways to shift the tone of a scene
without changing the structure.

## Subcategories

- **Dresses & Skirts**
- **Tops**
- **Pants**
- **Lingerie**
- **Socks & Stockings**
- **Heels & Boots**
- **Fetish & Roleplay** - latex, leather, harness wear, costume pieces
- **Gloves**

## Adding an item

For each item you add:

- A **subcategory** (from the list above).
- A **name** ("Black leather pencil skirt", "Latex bralette - red").
- An optional **note**: size, condition, comfort notes, restrictions.
- Optional **fetish tags**, picked from your own fetish list.

Once the item is saved you can also give it a **Use** rating of one to
three hearts, and switch it off entirely when you do not want it in
play.

The Domina reads the name and note verbatim. Be specific: she will
write tasks using the same vocabulary you used.

## How the Domina uses clothing

- **Preparation tasks** at the start of a scene: she names what she
  wants you in, and you put it on.
- **Material framing** for the whole session: a latex-heavy inventory
  pushes the Domina toward material-aware writing (texture, sound, the
  feel of putting it on).

Wardrobe does not change the pictures. A session image is the scene she
wrote, not a photo of what you have on.

## What counts as fetish wear

Latex, leather, harness wear, costume pieces: anything you would only
put on for a scene. If you have a piece you keep for serious nights,
that's where it goes.

The subcategory on its own does not make her reach for a piece more
often. What does is the fetish tags you put on it and the **Use**
rating you give it. Tag a latex dress with latex and it will surface in
the sessions built around latex.

## Items you do not own but want

Do not add items you cannot wear today. The Domina assumes any
inventory entry is fair game and will require it. If you want to set up
a wish list, do that outside MISTRIX; the inventory is for what is in
your wardrobe right now.

## Cross-reference

- **Fetishes > Clothing & Fetish Wear** lists the *fetish* categories
  (latex, leather, lingerie) you set interest levels on. Those are
  about what you find arousing.
- **Inventory > Clothing** lists the *specific items* you actually own.
  Those are what the Domina can require.

She uses both. Marking latex as Love and adding a single latex piece to
your inventory tells her you want latex in scenes and gives her
exactly one item to require.

# Accessories

The Accessories inventory tab covers everything that is neither a toy
nor a clothing item but is worn or used in a scene. This is where
restraints and ritual objects live.

## Subcategories

- **Restraints** - cuffs (leather, metal, padded), rope, hogtie sets,
  spreaders, ankle ties.
- **Collar & Leash** - collars (locking, posture, leather, metal),
  leashes.
- **Sensory** - blindfolds, hoods, white-noise earphones, sleep masks,
  anything that adds or removes sensation without being a toy.
- **Gags** - ball gags, bit gags, panel gags, ring gags, breathable
  hoods that count as gags.
- **Body** - chastity devices, harnesses, body jewellery used in scene,
  posture aids.

## Adding an item

For each item:

- A **subcategory** (from above).
- A **name** ("Padded leather wrist cuffs - black, lockable").
- A **note** for size, key location, time limits, or any caveat.
- Optional **fetish tags**, picked from your own fetish list.

Once the item is saved you can also give it a **Use** rating of one to
three hearts, and switch it off entirely when you do not want it in
play.

Notes matter most in this tab. The Domina reads them for sizing
("medium plug only", "tight fit on the ring gag") and for safety limits
("no longer than 30 minutes").

## Restraints and safety

The Domina will not coach you through restraint safety. Read up on:

- Circulation checks for limb cuffs.
- Quick-release options - a snip-cutter near the bed for rope, a
  spare key for locking cuffs.
- Spotter or check-in protocols for heavier restraint scenes.

The app expects you to know this. It enforces hard limits and the safe
word; it does not replace IRL technique training.

## Chastity

If you wear a chastity device under your Domina's control, add it
here. The Domina then:

- References it across sessions ("you are still locked").
- Plans denial-themed sessions around it.
- Respects any **time limits** in your note. If you wrote "no longer
  than 7 days" she will not push past that.

## Collars

Collars are stored here, but their **meaning** is set on the
[Fetishes > BDSM](../fetishes/bdsm.md) interest list (Collaring - Private,
Collaring - Public). The Domina treats collaring as a ritual when the
fetish is at Love level and a collar item is in your inventory.

## Items you do not own

Same rule as the other tabs: only add accessories you can use today.
The Domina assumes the list is current.

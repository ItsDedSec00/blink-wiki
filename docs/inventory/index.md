# Inventory

Your inventory is the catalogue of items the Domina knows you have. She
will not demand something you do not own; she works from the items you
have switched on.

## Pages in this section

- [Toys](./toys.md) - the five toy categories and what counts.
- [Clothing](./clothing.md) - dresses, lingerie, fetish wear, heels and boots, more.
- [Accessories](./accessories.md) - restraints, gags, collars, sensory gear.

## How she uses the inventory

When planning a session, the Domina picks from the items you have
switched on. She can mark an item as **required** (she will not run the
session without it) or **optional** (used if you say yes).

You will see two lists at the start of a session:

- **Bring out:** items required for this scene.
- **Have ready if you want:** optional items.

If you cannot use a required item right now, decline the proposal; she
will replan.

## What to add

You do not need to add every item you have ever owned. A useful
inventory contains:

- The toys you can actually reach right now.
- Clothing or lingerie you would put on for a scene tonight.
- Restraints, collars, and sensory items you have used at least once.

If you stop using something, remove it. The Domina assumes anything in
the list is fair game.

## Custom fields

Each entry supports a free-text **note**. Use it for sizing ("medium
plug only"), colour, material, or any caveat that affects whether she
should propose it. The Domina reads notes when she picks items.

Each entry also takes **fetish tags** from your own fetish list, and a
**Use** rating of one to three hearts. Tags tell her what the item is
for; the rating tells her how often you want it reached for.

## Limits

Free members can add 5 items per category. Premium and Premium Plus can
add 30 items in total across all three tabs.

She does not try to use everything at once. If your list is long she
picks a handful for a session and reaches for different ones next time,
so nothing sits unused.

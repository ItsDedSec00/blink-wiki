# Inventory

Your inventory is the catalogue of items the Domina knows you have. She
will not demand something you do not own; she works from the items you
have switched on.

## Pages in this section

- [Toys](./toys.md) - the five toy categories and what counts.
- [Clothing](./clothing.md) - the ten clothing subcategories, from casual wear and nightwear to lingerie, footwear and fetish wear.
- [Accessories](./accessories.md) - restraints, gags, collars, sensory gear.

## How she uses the inventory

Your inventory is not a checklist she hands you before a scene. It is
background she works from. She has the names of your switched on items
in front of her on every turn, and writes her directives around them.

- She will not invent equipment you do not own, and she names an item
  with your exact wording rather than paraphrasing it.
- The list is ordered by relevance. Items tagged with the theme she is
  working right now come first, then items tagged with any of your
  active interests, then the rest.
- She sees names only. When she needs to know what an item is for, she
  looks up its description and fetish tags.
- If she names something you cannot reach right now, say so in chat. She
  adapts in the same reply. There is no proposal sheet to decline.

## What to add

You do not need to add every item you have ever owned. A useful
inventory contains:

- The toys you can actually reach right now.
- Clothing or lingerie you would put on for a scene tonight.
- Restraints, collars, and sensory items you have used at least once.

If you stop using something, switch it off or delete it. The Domina
assumes anything switched on is fair game.

## Item fields

Each entry has five fields:

- **Subcategory**, required, picked from the list for that tab.
- **Name**, required. She reads it literally and says it back word for
  word, so write it the way you want her to say it.
- **Description**, optional, up to 500 characters. Sizing ("medium plug
  only"), colour, material, or any caveat that affects whether she
  should reach for it. She does not carry it on every turn; she looks it
  up when she needs to know what the item is for.
- **Image link**, optional. Puts a thumbnail on the item row.
- **Fetish tags**, optional. Start typing and it autocompletes against
  the fetish catalogue.

Tags are worth the extra ten seconds. They tell her what each item is
*for*, so when a scene turns to a theme you tagged, she reaches for the
matching item instead of picking one at random, and tagged items move to
the front of the list she sees. You can change them later from the tag
button on any item row.

Each row also carries a **Use** rating of one to three hearts, two by
default. Three hearts tells her to reach for that item often, one heart
tells her to use it sparingly.

## Limits

Free members can add 5 items per category. Premium and Premium Plus can
add 30 items in total across all three tabs.

The Add item card shows a live counter and refuses once you are at the
limit. Free members at the cap are offered an upgrade; paid members get
a note asking them to delete something first.

Because the cap is 30, she sees your whole switched on list every turn.
She never has to guess whether you own something. What changes between
turns is the order she sees it in.

## Backing up your inventory

At the top of the Inventory screen are **Export** and **Import**.

- **Export** saves your whole inventory, every tab, as a file. The
  button shows how many items it will write. Names, subcategories,
  descriptions, image links and fetish tags are included. The include
  switch and the Use hearts are not.
- **Import** reads one back. It reports what it did, for example
  "12 imported · 3 skipped (2 duplicates, 1 subcategory not in
  catalog)". Duplicates are skipped rather than doubled, and on the free
  tier any row that would push a category past 5 items is skipped too.

This is worth doing once. Your inventory is encrypted with your PIN, so
if you ever forget the PIN, this file is the only copy you can still
read. The account-wide [Data export](../account/data-export.md) does
not help here: it carries your inventory in its locked form, and
nothing but your PIN opens that. See
[PIN and Privacy](../safety/pin-and-privacy.md).

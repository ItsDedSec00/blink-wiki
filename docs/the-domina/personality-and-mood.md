# Personality and Mood

A Domina's personality has six fixed fields and one drifting field. The
fixed fields are set when she is created. The drifting field is her
mood, and every Domina has one.

## Fixed personality fields

| Field | Type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | strict / cold / warm / playful / ceremonial | strict |
| Speech style | formal / commanding / poetic / clipped | commanding |
| Intensity | 1 to 5 | 4 |
| Traits | freeform tags | patient, exacting, methodical |
| Specialties | up to five fetishes | chastity, edging, orgasm denial |
| Image aesthetic | freeform style anchor | "minimalist black studio, sharp light, no fabric" |

How these combine:

- **Tone + speech style** decide her voice.
- **Intensity** decides her default difficulty ceiling. A level 2 user
  with an intensity 5 Domina still sees gentle sessions, but they will
  feel pressured. A level 9 user with an intensity 2 Domina sees harder
  content than the number suggests because the Domina pushes the upper
  end of what intensity 2 allows.
- **Traits** flavour her word choice without changing the structure.
- **Specialties** bias which fetishes (from your interests) she leans
  into.
- **Image aesthetic** shapes how her pictures look. Together with her
  character shot it holds her visual style steady across sessions.

## Mood

Every Domina has a mood, preset and personal alike. Each one tracks
hers with you separately, so a bad run with one does not follow you to
another. Mood is one of three values:

- **Pleased** - she is warm, encouraging, generous with praise.
- **Neutral** - default.
- **Displeased** - she is coolly exacting; her expectations are high;
  less praise.

### How mood changes

Mood moves on two mechanisms: the **verdict** she passes at the end of
a session, and **gradual decay toward neutral** the longer you are
away.

| You did this | Effect on mood |
|---|---|
| Finished a session she scored 5, Exemplary | Strong shift toward Pleased |
| Finished a session she scored 4, Good | Shift toward Pleased |
| Finished a session she scored 3, Adequate | No change |
| Finished a session she scored 2, Weak | Shift toward Displeased |
| Finished a session she scored 1, Poor | Strong shift toward Displeased |
| Tapped the safe word | Resets to Neutral |
| Time passes between sessions | Mood drifts back toward Neutral, about one point a day |

Her verdict is her own judgement of how you served, not the rating you
gave the session. See
[After the session](../sessions/after-the-session.md). Ordinary chat
between sessions nudges her as well, but it takes several turns in the
same direction to move her a single step.

After a few days away, a Pleased or Displeased mood settles back to
Neutral on its own. Walking away from a session is not punished: there
is no separate penalty for leaving one unfinished.

### Mood does not override hard limits

Even displeased she still respects every hard limit. Mood changes
voice, not allowed content.

## Resetting personality

You cannot reset a preset Domina; she ships as-is. You **can** edit a
personal Domina's fixed fields at any time from the Domina
Configurator. Changes take effect on the next session.

You **cannot** manually set mood. Mood drifts on its own based on the
table above. The fastest way to shift it is to finish a session and
serve her well in it.

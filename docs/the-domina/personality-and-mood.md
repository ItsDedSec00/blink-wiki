# Personality and Mood

A Domina's personality has a set of fixed fields and one drifting
field. You set the fixed fields yourself when you build a personal
Domina; a preset ships with hers already set. The drifting field is her
mood, and every Domina has one.

## Fixed personality fields

These are the choices the builder walks you through.

| Field | Options | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Style | caring / sensual / strict / playful / ceremonial / sadistic | strict |
| Role | mommy / mentor / queen / mistress / goddess / sadist | mistress |
| Strictness | 1 to 5, loving to strict | 4 |
| Tone | warm / nurturing / teasing / playful / strict / cold / ceremonial / poetic | strict |
| Speech style | gentle / formal / commanding / conversational / poetic / clipped | commanding |
| Intensity | 1 to 5, gentle to extreme | 4 |
| Traits | up to 6, picked from a fixed list of 30 | patient, exacting, methodical |
| Specialties | up to 5 fetishes, each weighted 1 to 3 hearts | chastity, edging, orgasm denial |
| Visual Aesthetic | freetext style anchor | "minimalist black studio, sharp light, no fabric" |

How these combine:

- **Style + role** set the archetype she plays.
- **Tone + speech style** decide her voice.
- **Intensity** decides how severe her demands are and how she sounds
  giving them. At 1 or 2 she is measured and checks in often. At 4 she
  is severe and drops the softening words. At 5 she is cold and does
  not negotiate small things. It is not a ceiling on content.
- **Strictness** is a separate dial for warmth. Low strictness softens
  her tone, never her demands. High strictness rations praise. At 3 it
  changes nothing either way.
- **Your level** is what decides how far she pushes. She matches what
  she demands to your experience, you decide where your limit is, and
  going soft when you asked for more counts as her failing you. Your
  hard limits are the only boundary on content.
- **Traits** flavour her word choice without changing the structure.
- **Specialties** bias which fetishes she leans into. The hearts set
  how central each one is to her. Your hard limits always override a
  specialty.
- **Visual Aesthetic** shapes how her pictures look. Together with her
  character shot it holds her visual style steady.

## Mood

Every Domina has a mood, preset and personal alike. Each one tracks
hers with you separately, so a bad run with one does not follow you to
another. Mood is one of three values:

- **Pleased** - she shows it in her own register. On a warm Domina that
  is open praise; on a sadistic one it is appetite and sharper play.
  Pleased never means she goes easier on you.
- **Neutral** - default.
- **Displeased** - she withholds warmth and praise. You earn her
  approval back through obedience before she softens.

### How mood changes

The main lever is the **verdict** she passes at the end of a session.
Everything else either resets her or drifts her back toward neutral.

| You did this | Effect on mood |
|---|---|
| Finished a session she scored 5, Exemplary | Strong shift toward Pleased |
| Finished a session she scored 4, Good | Shift toward Pleased |
| Finished a session she scored 3, Adequate | No change |
| Finished a session she scored 2, Weak | Shift toward Displeased |
| Finished a session she scored 1, Poor | Strong shift toward Displeased |
| Refused a tribute she demanded | Strong shift toward Displeased |
| Tapped the safe word | Resets to Neutral |
| Time passes between sessions | Mood drifts back toward Neutral, about one point a day |

Her verdict is her own judgement of how you served, not the rating you
gave the session. See
[After the session](../sessions/after-the-session.md). Ordinary chat
between sessions nudges her as well, but it takes several turns in the
same direction to move her a single step.

After a few days away, a Pleased or Displeased mood settles back to
Neutral on its own. Walking away from a session is not punished: there
is no separate penalty for leaving one unfinished.

### Mood does not override hard limits

Even displeased she still respects every hard limit. Mood changes
voice, not allowed content.

## Resetting personality

You cannot reset a preset Domina; she ships as-is. You **can** edit a
personal Domina's fixed fields at any time. Open **Dominas**, tap the
cog on her card, and pick the section you want to change. Changes take
effect on her very next message, not on the next session.

The cog opens the Domina you are editing as your active one. If you tap
it on a personal Domina who was not active, she becomes active.

You **cannot** manually set mood. Mood drifts on its own based on the
table above. The fastest way to shift it is to finish a session and
serve her well in it.

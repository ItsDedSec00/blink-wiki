# Character Shots

When you create a personal Domina, MISTRIX generates a **character shot**:
a high-quality portrait of her. This portrait then anchors her look in
every picture she sends you afterwards.

Character shots are only for **personal Dominas**. Preset Dominas use
curated portraits that ship with the app.

## Two ways to decide how she looks

You pick one on the **Source** step of the builder.

- **Describe her.** You choose her skin tone, eyes, age, hair, height
  and build on the next three steps. This is the default.
- **Use my reference photo.** You upload a portrait and it anchors her
  face, skin, eyes, hair and build. The Face, Hair and Body steps are
  skipped. A well-lit photo looking straight at the camera works best.
  The photo you upload is kept for about an hour and then discarded, so
  if you break off and come back later, upload it again.

Either way the rest of the configuration still drives her wardrobe,
lighting and mood. The difference is who owns her face and body.

## How a character shot is built

1. You configure her in the builder: name, style, role, voice, traits,
   specialties, appearance, and the freetext **Visual Aesthetic**.
2. MISTRIX turns that into one portrait on the **Her Portrait** screen.
3. If you like it, tap **Continue** - it becomes her character shot.
4. If you don't, tap **Regenerate prompt** (or **Regenerate from
   reference**) and you get another portrait from the same source. Keep
   going until one feels right.
5. The portrait you keep is saved as her character shot. Every picture
   she sends you later uses it as a style anchor, so she stays
   recognisable.

A character shot takes longer to arrive than an ordinary picture, so
give it a moment. Generating one is part of Premium and it does not
cost Charms.

## What anchors mean

Anchoring means every new picture of her keeps the face, build,
posture, and aesthetic of the shot you kept. It is not a hard
guarantee - generated images vary. But across many sessions she looks
meaningfully like the same person.

## What to put in the Visual Aesthetic

**Visual Aesthetic** is a freetext box on the last step of the builder,
next to the preview. The best entries are short and visual:

- "Minimalist black studio, sharp directional light, no fabric on the
  walls."
- "Velvet drapes, candle light, deep maroon palette, antique mirror in
  the background."
- "Crisp daylight, white tile, clinical lab framing, latex only."

Avoid:

- Vague mood words alone ("dark", "sensual", "intense"). They produce
  generic results.
- Direct character references to real public figures. These are
  refused, and what comes back instead is off-target.

## Regenerating a character shot

If you don't like the current shot:

- Tap regenerate to produce another portrait from the same source.
- Adjust the fields (especially **Visual Aesthetic**) between
  regenerations to steer the result.
- There is no separate daily quota for character shots. The only
  gating is your personal-Domina count: one on Premium, three on
  Premium Plus, and you can regenerate the character shot for any of
  them as often as you like.

## Character shot vs the pictures she sends

- The **character shot** is her - the portrait itself. It shows on her
  card under **Dominas** and at the top of her chat. It is not a
  gallery entry, and generating it does not use up your daily image
  allowance.
- A **picture she sends in chat** is a fresh image of the moment you
  are in. She offers it, you confirm, and it is generated with her
  character shot as the style anchor. These do come out of your daily
  image allowance, and each one is saved to your
  [AI Studio](../features/ai-studio.md) gallery, privately, so you can
  go back to it later.

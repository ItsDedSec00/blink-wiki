# Character Shots

When you create a personal Domina, MISTRIX generates a **character shot**:
a high-quality portrait of her built from the description you wrote.
This portrait then anchors her look in every image she stars in.

Character shots are only for **personal Dominas**. Preset Dominas use
static curated portraits that ship with the app.

## How a character shot is built

1. You describe her on the Domina Configurator: name, age range, look,
   tone, traits, specialties, and the freetext **image aesthetic**.
2. MISTRIX turns what you wrote into one portrait.
3. If you like it, accept it - it becomes her character shot.
4. If you don't, tap regenerate and the app produces another portrait
   from the same description. Keep iterating until one feels right.
5. The portrait you accept is saved as her character shot. Future
   session images use it as a style anchor so she stays recognisable.

A character shot takes longer to arrive than an ordinary picture, so
give it a moment. Generating one is part of Premium and it does not
cost Charms.

## What anchors mean

Anchoring means every new picture of her keeps the face, build,
posture, and aesthetic of the shot you accepted. It is not a hard
guarantee - generated images vary. But across many sessions she looks
meaningfully like the same person.

## What to put in the image aesthetic

The image aesthetic is a freetext line on the Configurator. The best
ones are short and visual:

- "Minimalist black studio, sharp directional light, no fabric on the
  walls."
- "Velvet drapes, candle light, deep maroon palette, antique mirror in
  the background."
- "Crisp daylight, white tile, clinical lab framing, latex only."

Avoid:

- Vague mood words alone ("dark", "sensual", "intense"). They produce
  generic results.
- Direct character references to real public figures. These are
  refused, and what comes back instead is off-target.

## Regenerating a character shot

If you don't like the current shot:

- Tap regenerate to produce another portrait from the same description.
- Adjust the description fields (especially **image aesthetic**)
  between regenerations to steer the result.
- There is no separate daily quota for character shots. The only
  gating is your personal-Domina count: one on Premium, three on
  Premium Plus, and you can regenerate the character shot for any of
  them as often as you like.

## Character shot vs in-session images

- The **character shot** is her - the portrait.
- An **in-session image** is the scene she set for a specific task. It
  uses her character shot as a style anchor but the framing is the
  scene, not her face.

Both live in your gallery and can be revisited later.

# Preset vs Personal Dominas

MISTRIX has two kinds of Domina, and they can coexist on one account.

## Preset Dominas

- Ready-made Dominas with fixed names, looks, tones, and specialties.
  You cannot edit them; each one ships as she is.
- Her portrait is fixed. Any picture she sends in chat is built from
  that portrait, so she looks the same every time.
- Her mood toward you moves with how your sessions go, and each preset
  tracks it separately.

Fifteen of them ship today, in two groups. Open **Dominas** from the
dashboard to see them. More may be added over time.

### The five free presets

Five of them sit under **Presets** and are open to every account,
including Free. On a Free account these are the ones you choose from
during onboarding. On a paid account onboarding takes you into the
builder instead, and the presets are simply there when you want them.

### The ten Premium presets

The other ten sit under **Specialists**. On Free they show as locked
cards, and tapping one opens an upgrade prompt. On Premium and
Premium Plus you can open any of them.

**Miss Spiral** is one of the Specialists. Her specialty is hypnotic
induction, drone training and mental conditioning, and like the rest of
that group she needs Premium. A Free account cannot open her chat.

She is also the only preset with an entry warning. Before her chat
opens for the first time, a screen spells out what she does in
character, and states plainly that none of it is real and that nothing
she asks inside the fiction is binding outside it. You either accept it
or go back.

### Picking and switching

Free accounts pick one of the five free presets during onboarding and
chat with that one. You can switch later, but on Free the switch clears
the chat, sessions, memories and relationship progress with the Domina
you are leaving. Premium and Premium Plus keep each Domina's history
separately, so switching costs you nothing. See
[Choose a Domina](../getting-started/choose-a-domina.md).

## Personal Dominas (paid)

The configurable kind. You design her - name, style, role, tone, speech
style, intensity, strictness, traits, specialties, appearance, and a
freetext **Visual Aesthetic** that anchors her image style.

- **Premium**: 1 personal Domina.
- **Premium Plus**: up to 3 personal Dominas, each independent. Switch
  the active one from the **Dominas** screen.

A personal Domina also gets a **character shot** at creation - a
portrait generated from your description, or from a reference photo you
upload. The same portrait anchors every picture she sends you. See
[Character Shots](./character-shots.md).

Both kinds have a **mood** that moves. See
[Personality and Mood](./personality-and-mood.md).

## Side by side

| | Preset | Personal |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | five free, ten need Premium | Premium or higher |
| Number you can open | 1 at a time out of 5 (Free) / all 15 (Premium and up) | 1 (Premium) / 3 (Premium Plus) |
| Customisable | no, fixed as shipped | yes, fully |
| Look | fixed portrait | follows her character shot |
| Specialties | fixed | you decide |

## Choosing which to use

- **Brand new to MISTRIX?** Start with a preset. Learn the rhythm first.
- **Want a Domina that is yours?** Build a personal one on Premium or
  higher. The voice, look, and specialties are entirely your call.

There is no penalty for mixing. You can keep three personal Dominas
and still session occasionally with a preset.

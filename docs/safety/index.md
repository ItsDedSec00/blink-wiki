# Safety

MISTRIX is designed so the stop signal is always closer than the next
task. If you are unsure about anything, the safe word is one tap away.

## The three guarantees

1. **The safe word always works.** During a session the Safe Word button
   sits in the chat header, and a full-screen page you open carries its
   own copy. Outside a session it is the first button in the footer of
   the chat menu. Tapping it ends the scene immediately. You do not lose
   your data; you do not earn XP for that session; her mood resets to
   neutral rather than turning against you.
2. **Hard limits are absolute.** Anything on your hard limits list binds
   the Domina in everything she plans and writes for you: she never
   mentions it, references it, or implies it. Hard limits cannot be
   overridden by a clever message, a mood shift, or a future update. See
   [Hard limits explained](./hard-limits-explained.md).
3. **We cannot read your sensitive data.** Your fetishes, hard limits,
   inventory, safe word, the text of your routines and open tasks, your
   goal and standing rules, and your chat messages are encrypted on your
   device with a key derived from your PIN. We only ever hold the
   scrambled version. Lose the PIN, lose the data - even support cannot
   read it. Some things sit outside that boundary on purpose, because the
   app has to read them to run: your profile details, your sessions and
   their summaries, your images, your AI Studio prompts, your Domina
   herself and what she remembers about you.
   [PIN and privacy](./pin-and-privacy.md) has the full list.

## What the app watches for on its own

- **Before your first session**, a one-time notice explains that every
  directive is written by an AI, that it cannot see you and does not know
  your health, that adapting or skipping a directive is always allowed,
  and that pain, numbness or dizziness means stop.
- **Under the message box**, a line on every chat screen repeats it: all
  replies are AI-generated and may contain errors, so check every
  directive before you act on it.
- **After a reply is written**, MISTRIX checks it for directives that
  would be risky to follow as written: breathing and the airway, pressure
  on the neck, cut-off circulation, restraint with no release in reach,
  harmful substances, burns, unsafe insertion, and clamps left on too
  long. When it finds one, a crimson **Safety Notice** appears under her
  message. For clamps it names the number: no more than 15 minutes at a
  time, off at once if the area goes numb or loses colour.

Her words are never rewritten and never hidden. The notice sits outside
her message because it is the app speaking, not her.

## Before your first heavier session

- Read [Aftercare](./aftercare.md). Plan it the same way you plan the
  scene.
- Pick a safe word you would not say by accident. Avoid "stop" and "no"
  if those are part of how you talk in scene.
- Tell someone in your life where you are if you live alone and the
  session involves restraints. MISTRIX is software; it is not a spotter.
- Read the [Hard Limits](../fetishes/hard-limits.md) page and re-check your
  list.

## If something goes wrong

- Use the safe word inside a session.
- Outside a session, you can clear chat history, change your active
  Domina, edit your interest levels, or hide a fetish from future
  planning - all from the app settings.
- If you experience harassment or content that should not exist, use the
  **Report message** button on the Domina message that carries it. That
  covers a message with an image in it too. Reports go to moderation.
- If you want your account erased, see
  [Deleting your account](../account/deleting-your-account.md). There is a
  30-day grace window if you change your mind.

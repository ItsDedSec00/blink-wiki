# Hard Limits Explained

A hard limit is the strongest "no" in MISTRIX. It overrides interest
levels, mood, and any in-chat message you ever send. This page explains
why and how to use the system well.

## How they work

Whenever the Domina plans or writes anything - a session, a chat
message, a summary, the description behind an image - your current
hard-limits list binds her as an **absolute restriction**: never
mention, reference, or imply any of these.

She gets that rule every time, without exception, and it outranks
anything said in a scene, hers or yours. No clever message talks her
past it.

## What this means in practice

- Asking her to ignore a hard limit "just tonight" does not work.
- Telling her you changed your mind in chat does not work.
- Adding a synonym or euphemism for the limit does not work; the rule
  covers the topic, not just the words you happened to use.
- Switching to a different Domina does not work either; every Domina
  on your account sees the same hard limits.

If you want a hard limit removed, open **You > Preferences > Fetish
map** and take the cross off that entry. The change applies immediately
to all future sessions on all your Dominas.

## What is on the list

The hard-limits list is drawn from the same curated catalogue as the
fetish interest list. You cannot invent a hard limit out of free text
(the same way you cannot invent an interest); you pick from the
catalogue and mark it.

If something is missing from the catalogue that you would want to
hard-limit, contact support - the catalogue evolves over time.

## Limits beyond your list

Underneath your personal list there is a floor that applies to every
account and that nobody can switch off. Anything sexual involving
minors, and anything otherwise illegal, is refused. Emptying your hard
limits does not unlock it, and neither does any setting in MISTRIX.

Your hard limits live on top of that floor. They cover the additional
content categories that the curated catalogue lets you mark as
off-limits for you personally.

## How to keep the list useful

- **Re-read it every couple of months.** What felt edgy a year ago may
  be fine now. What felt fine may be a no now. The list is yours to
  curate.
- **Add things that you would not enjoy as a surprise.** Even if you
  would never opt in, marking them as hard limits is belt-and-braces
  protection.
- **Subtract things you have genuinely changed your mind on.** Keeping
  stale hard limits is fine, but it does narrow what she can plan for
  you.

## Hard limits vs the safe word

Hard limits stop the Domina from ever bringing something up. The safe
word stops the *current scene*. Both exist for a reason:

- Hard limits prevent the topic from appearing at all.
- Safe word handles surprise - the case where something happening now
  isn't working, regardless of whether the topic was permitted.

Use both. Curate the list. Tap the button.

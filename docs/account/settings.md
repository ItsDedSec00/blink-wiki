# Account Settings

Tap **You** in the bottom bar to open your account screen. It holds
everything about you that is not inside a session. Some things are
edited here, some open a screen of their own, and a few cannot be
changed at all.

The screen is one column of groups, always in this order:

1. Your profile card.
2. **My Domina**
3. **Preferences**
4. **App**
5. **This phone**, only inside the companion app.
6. **Android app**
7. **Privacy & Security**
8. **Plan**
9. **Community & About**, which ends with the two red sign-out rows.

The version you are running is printed under the last group.

## Profile

The card at the top shows your name, your tier and your level. Tap it
to edit your profile. That screen holds four fields, all optional. The
three below them are shown here for completeness and are not on it.

| Field | Editable | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Name / how to address you** | yes | What the Domina calls you. Up to 40 characters. |
| **Gender** | yes | Male, Female, Non-binary, Other, or Prefer not to say. Affects how she refers to you. |
| **Biological sex** | yes | Penis, Vagina, or Prefer not to say. Shapes which physical tasks she gives you. |
| **Age** | yes | One number, 18 or older. Used to shape her framing. It is not an age check. |
| Username | no | Chosen when you signed up. Ask support if you need it changed. |
| Email | no | The address you sign in with. You cannot change it here; ask support if you need it changed. |
| Level | no | Starts from the questions you answered when you joined; rises with every message and every finished session. She pitches how hard she pushes in chat to it. |

## App

| Row | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Bottom bar** | Choose which two screens sit in your bottom bar. Home, Chat and You always stay. |
| **Notifications** | Turns her messages on or off on this device. |

The **Notifications** row reads **On**, **Off**, or **Not registered**.
"Not registered" means this device asked for notifications but is not
set up to receive them, so nothing can reach you. A warning appears
under the row with a **Register this device** button that fixes it.

On iPhone and iPad the row says "Install the app to enable" instead.
Notifications there only work once MISTRIX is on your home screen. Tap
the row for the steps.

### How often she writes

With notifications on, a **Frequency** control appears under the row.

| Setting | What you get | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **Low** | At most one message a day, with long quiet stretches. | All |
| **Normal** | Up to three a day, and never right after you were active. | All |
| **High** | Up to five a day, with shorter pauses in between. | Premium, Premium Plus |

On Free, **High** is shown with a small lock. Tapping it opens an
upgrade prompt instead of changing the setting. If you had High and
your plan ends, the control reads Normal again and your choice comes
back when you upgrade.

She stays quiet between 22:00 and 09:00 in your local time. Anything
due in those hours arrives the next morning. Reminders you set up
yourself for routines and tasks do not count towards the daily number.

### Delivery check

Under the frequency control sits **Delivery check** with a **Send
test** button. It sends one notification right now to every device you
have switched notifications on for, then tells you what happened: it
went out, this device is not registered, this device's registration had
expired and was just removed, or the message was refused.

Use it when notifications are switched on but nothing ever reaches you.
It tells you whether the message left at all. If it says it was sent
and you still see nothing, your phone or computer is hiding it, and the
setting to change is there rather than here. There is a ten-second
wait between tests.

## This phone

## Android app

Next comes **Android app**, and what it shows depends on where you
opened MISTRIX.

In a browser it offers **Download Mistrix Companion** with the version,
the size and when it was released, plus two buttons:

| Button | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Stable** | The tested release. This is the one to take. |
| **Nightly** | The newest version, put out each day and not tested. It can crash, lose your settings, or refuse to open. |

The app is not in the Play Store, so Android asks you once whether to
allow the install. **Nightly** warns you before it downloads, because
going back to Stable means uninstalling first, and that takes
everything the app holds with it.

Inside the companion app the same section shows the version you are on,
whether a newer one exists (with a **Get it** button), and a **Release
channel** switch between Stable and Nightly.

### Repair locked data

Once you have a PIN, tapping **PIN & encryption** opens a panel, and
**Repair locked data** is a button inside it. If the row reads "Not
set", the tap takes you to PIN setup instead and there is no repair
button anywhere. The check asks whether everything you have saved can
still be opened with the PIN you use now.

Most people never need it. It matters when something was saved under a
PIN you no longer have. What you notice is a part of the app that stays
empty or will not save.

The button is deliberately not there while a PIN change of yours is
unfinished. That data is not stranded, only half-moved, so the panel
offers to finish the change instead. Finish it and it reads again.

You can also meet the list without asking for it. If you start a PIN
change and something cannot be unlocked, the check runs first and shows
you the list before the change goes ahead.

If everything is readable, it says so and nothing happens. If something
is not, you get a plain list of what is affected - profile details,
fetish map, learned fetish interests, hard limits, inventory, safe
word, chat guidance, chat history, her memories - and two choices:

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| **Keep** | Nothing is touched. The listed items stay unreadable. |
| **Discard & rebuild** | The listed items are deleted for good, so you can set them up again from scratch. |

**Discard & rebuild** stays greyed out until you type `DISCARD` into
the box above it. Discarding cannot be undone, and it never touches
anything that is still readable. Run the check and read the list before
you decide.

## Community and about

The last group holds **Help & support** (Discord or email),
**Suggestions**, **What's new**, the **Terms of Service** and the
**Privacy Policy**, and then the two sign-out rows below. The version
you are running is printed under the group. Quote it when you report a
problem.

## Signing out

The two sign-out rows are the last two rows of the **Community & About**
group, and both are red.

| Row | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sign out** | Signs out this device only. Your other devices stay signed in. |
| **Sign out everywhere** | Ends every session on every device, this one included. |

**Sign out everywhere** asks you to confirm first. When you do, every
device signed in to your account is signed out at once, the one in your
hand as well, and you have to sign in again right here. Nothing is
deleted: your chats, images and PIN are exactly as you left them.

Use it if you are still signed in somewhere you no longer control, or
you think somebody else got into your account. If a device was taken,
change your PIN afterwards too, so your saved data is locked with a new
key. You can use it up to five times an hour; past that you are asked
to wait before the next try.

## What you can't change from settings

- Username - ask support.
- Email - ask support.
- Level - it follows your XP, and XP follows your chatting and your
  sessions.
- Past sessions - there is no screen that edits or deletes a single
  one. They are removed automatically 365 days after they started.
- The Domina's mood - it drifts, you don't set it.
- Universal hard limits - they apply whatever your own list says.

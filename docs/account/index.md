# Account

Everything to do with your account, your PIN, and your data lives here.

## Pages in this section

- [Settings](./settings.md) - profile fields, level, safe word, display preferences.
- [PIN and recovery](./pin-and-recovery.md) - changing your PIN, what happens if you forget it.
- [Data export](./data-export.md) - download every personal record we hold for you (GDPR Art. 20).
- [Deleting your account](./deleting-your-account.md) - the 30-day grace window and how to cancel.
- [Password reset](./password-reset.md) - getting a reset link by email.

## What we store about you

- The fields you typed in: display name, age, gender if you set it,
  level, safe word, your interest levels, your hard limits, your
  inventory, your custom Domina configuration.
- Generated artifacts: session plans, session text, summaries, generated
  images, AI Studio images.
- Operational data: your tier, whether the account has been suspended,
  the date you joined, and when you were last active.
- What you paid and when. We never see or hold your card number.

The most sensitive fields are encrypted with a key derived from your
PIN, so we cannot read them. See
[PIN and Privacy](../safety/pin-and-privacy.md).

## Your rights

- **See it:** download a JSON of everything via [Data export](./data-export.md).
- **Take it back:** delete the account; we wipe it after 30 days unless
  you cancel.
- **Change it:** edit your profile, fetishes, limits, and inventory at
  any time. Username changes go through support.

## What you cannot change yourself

- **Username.** Globally unique and audit-relevant; contact support.
- **Email.** It is the address you sign in with; create a new account
  if you need a different one.
- **Past session text.** Once a session is complete, the text and
  summary are part of the record.

# Data Export

You can download a JSON file holding every personal record MISTRIX
keeps about you, at any time, no questions asked. This is your right
under GDPR Article 20 (data portability) and MISTRIX honours it as a
self-service action.

## How to export

- Open **You > Privacy & Security**.
- Tap **Export my data**. The row reads **Preparing export…** while it
  is put together.
- Your browser saves a JSON file with your username and the date in
  its name.

There is no limit. You can re-export as often as you want.

## What is in the file

Everything we hold under your account:

- Your profile: name, tier, level and XP, Charm balance, safe word,
  language, and the dates on the account.
- Your fetish map, including how your interests have shifted with use.
- Your hard limits.
- Your inventory.
- Your personal Domina(s) and their personality settings.
- Your sessions: plan, text, summary, rating, dates.
- Your chat messages.
- What she remembers about you.
- Your generated images, and your AI Studio images and videos with
  their prompts and whether you published them.
- Your routines, your one-off tasks, and what you completed.
- Your Chaster connection, if you have one.
- Your Charm ledger, your referrals and your coupons.
- Your Expose posts, votes and reactions, and your Findom settings.
- Your reports and suggestions.
- The devices you paired and the messages still waiting to reach them.

## What you cannot read directly

Some of it comes out readable and some of it comes out locked.
Anything your PIN locks stays locked in the file. We cannot open it,
so the export cannot open it either. To read those parts outside the
app you would have to unlock them with your PIN yourself.

That is not a problem in practice, because the readable version of all
of it is in the app, on the screen it belongs to, once you have
unlocked with your PIN. The export is for keeping a copy, checking
what we hold, and answering a regulator.

## What is not in the file

- Other users (you do not have personal records on other people in
  MISTRIX; the app is single-player).
- The image and video files themselves. They are listed with their
  prompts and dates, not packed into the JSON.
- Credentials: what your devices need to receive her messages, and the
  access we hold for your Chaster account. Those are secrets, not
  records about you.
- Billing details. Invoices live on the billing page you reach from
  **You > Plan > Subscription**.

## File size

Typical files are a few hundred KB to a few MB. Heavy AI Studio users
with many generations can produce larger files, because every
generation is listed with its prompt.

## Privacy after export

Once the JSON is on your device, it's your responsibility. Treat it as
sensitive. The export contains your username, every session you ever
ran, every message you sent, and the locked copies of your most
sensitive configuration.

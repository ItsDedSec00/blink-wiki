# Password Reset

Your password is the thing you sign in with. It is not your PIN. If
you have lost it, we send a reset link to your email address.

## If you signed up with Google

Then you have no MISTRIX password and there is nothing to reset. You
sign in with **Continue with Google**, and a password cannot be added
to the account later. If you cannot get into that Google account any
more, you cannot get into MISTRIX either, and support cannot move the
account to a different sign-in.

The rest of this page is for accounts created with an email address and
a password.

## How to reset

1. Open the MISTRIX sign-in screen.
2. Type the email address on your account into the email field.
3. Tap **Forgot password?**.
4. Check your inbox for the reset link.
5. Open the link and set a new password.
6. Sign in with the new password.

The link is single-use and expires after about an hour. If it expires,
ask for another one.

## What does NOT happen during a password reset

- Your **PIN** is untouched. Password and PIN are independent. See
  [PIN and Privacy](../safety/pin-and-privacy.md).
- Your **sessions, history, level, XP, Charm balance, and tier** are
  all untouched.
- Your **personal Domina(s)** are untouched.

A password reset only changes how you sign in. Nothing about your
account state changes.

## What to do if you do not receive the email

- Check your spam folder. Reset mails are often filtered.
- The mail can arrive from a google.com address rather than from
  mistrix.ai, so search your inbox for that too.
- Confirm you typed the email address that is actually on the MISTRIX
  account.
- If you still see nothing, contact support. We can confirm whether an
  account exists for that address, but we cannot set a new password for
  you. The link in the email is the only way.

## Changing the password without forgetting it

There is no form for typing the old password and a new one. Changing
it uses the same reset link:

1. Sign in normally.
2. Open **You > Privacy & Security**.
3. Tap **Reset password**. The row reads **Reset email sent** once it
   is on its way.
4. Open the link in your inbox and set the new password.

## What if I can't access the email account?

Then you cannot reset it on your own. The link is the only way, and it
only ever goes to the address on the account.

Your options are:

- Recover the email account itself.
- Ask support to move your account to an address you can reach, then
  run the reset as normal. Support can change the address; support
  cannot set a password for you and cannot hand you a reset link.
- Start fresh with a new MISTRIX account on a new email. The old
  account will eventually stop being used; you can schedule deletion if
  you want it gone, but you'd need access to sign in for that.

## Security note

A password has to be at least 8 characters. Nothing checks whether you
have used it somewhere else, so pick one you do not reuse: reuse is a
real risk for any account that contains sensitive data, MISTRIX
included.

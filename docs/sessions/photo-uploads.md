# Photo Uploads

You can send the Domina a photo at any point in a session, and between
sessions too. The camera icon sits next to the message input; on a
narrow screen it is inside the **+** menu, under **Or send a photo**.
From there you can take a photo or pick one you already have.

## How the Domina uses your photo

A large picture is no problem. Your device shrinks it to about 1024
pixels on the long side before it is sent, so the upload stays quick.
Very fine detail does not survive that, so frame what you want her to
notice rather than relying on a distant close-up. She reads what is in
the picture and answers with a directive shaped by what she saw.

The Domina is honest about the medium: she does not pretend the photo
is live video. She is reacting to a still image.

A photo can be up to 10 MB. Send them one at a time. If you fire two
off at once you will be asked to wait a moment before the next.

## What she will comment on

- Posture and position.
- Visible items (cuffs, collar, plug, clothing).
- Apparent state of compliance with the previous task.
- General framing if you sent a wide shot of the scene.

## What she will not do

- Identify you, anyone else, or your location.
- Comment on hard-limit content even if you sent a photo of it.
- React with sensory specifics she could not see ("you smell like").

## When to send

- **After a posture task.** The next directive can be specific.
- **After equipment swap.** "Show the new restraint" - she confirms it.
- **For proof of completion** when she asked for it explicitly.

## When not to send

- For greeting or warm-up. She does not need a face shot.
- When you are unsure whether the photo would land safely in your phone
  gallery and the MISTRIX image gallery. It is stored.
- When the photo is too dark or off-target - she will just produce a
  generic reply.

## Storage

A photo you send is kept with your account. It appears in your gallery
alongside her generated images, and she can look at it again later if
you point her back at it. You can delete any photo from the gallery at
any time, and it is gone for good.

Photos are **not** locked behind your PIN the way your chat messages,
your interests and your limits are. That is the trade for keeping them
viewable in your gallery. If a photo would be a risk to keep, do not
send it.

## Free users

Photo upload is available to all tiers, including Free. The Domina will
not gate her tasks on photos for Free users - photos enrich the reply,
they do not block it.

# Using the Safe Word

The Safe Word is the single most important control in MISTRIX. The button
is always visible during an active session, in crimson, at the bottom
of the screen. One tap ends the scene.

## When to use it

Use the safe word when:

- You are not okay.
- You feel pressured beyond your comfort zone in a way you don't want
  to renegotiate inside the scene.
- Something physical is going wrong.
- You are losing the thread and want a clean stop, not a mid-session
  course correction.

You do not need a justification. The safe word is not a judgement call.

## When not to use it

For small adjustments inside a scene that you want to continue, write a
short message instead:

- "Slow this down."
- "Skip the next task."
- "Too intense - take it back a notch."

See [Writing Good Messages](../communication/writing-good-messages.md) for
patterns that work.

## What happens when you tap it

The instant you tap, the session is marked ended:

- The Domina ends the scene with a short, neutral acknowledgement.
- No XP is awarded.
- You are not asked to rate it, and she passes no verdict on it.
- Her mood resets to **neutral**. It does not drift to disappointed.
  This is the same for a preset Domina and a personal one.
- The session **does** count against your daily quota. On Free, that
  means a safeworded session uses up your one session for the day -
  there is no "free retry" via safeword. Use it when you need to, but
  knowing that.
- The session shows up in your history.

## After

You are back in the normal chat with your Domina. You can:

- Take a break.
- Chat with her about what didn't work.
- Start a fresh session whenever you are ready.

She will not push you. After a safeword, she leans toward gentle
follow-up unless you steer her elsewhere.

## The word itself

You set your safe word during onboarding. You can change it any time on
the **Profile** screen.

- Pick something you would not say by accident.
- Avoid words that are part of how you talk in scenes ("stop", "no",
  "please" are bad picks; "red" or "pineapple" are fine).
- The current word is shown on the button so you do not have to
  remember it. The button still works if you tap it without typing.

## The button is always visible

If at any point a session's UI seems to be hiding the safe word, treat
that as a bug and force-close the app. You can re-open and continue
from where you were; you cannot get stuck. The safe word is not
allowed to be hidden by design.

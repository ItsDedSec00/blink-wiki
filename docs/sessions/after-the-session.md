# After the Session

When you end a session and confirm it, you get the chance to rate it,
your Domina passes her own verdict, XP is awarded, a summary is
written, and a **Session Complete** card appears in the conversation.
Aftercare is offered on the same page if you want it.

## 1. The rating

The **End session** page carries a **1 to 5** rating before you confirm
the end. It is private feedback for us. It does not move your Domina's
mood, it does not change your XP, and she never reads it. You can end
the session without picking a number.

Rate honestly anyway. It is how we find out which sessions land and
which ones do not.

## 2. Her verdict

She judges the session herself, from the transcript and nothing else.
Her verdict appears on the Session Complete card as a score out of 5:

| Score | Label |
|---|---|
| 1 | Poor |
| 2 | Weak |
| 3 | Adequate |
| 4 | Good |
| 5 | Exemplary |

Underneath it she leaves one line addressed to you about how you
behaved. This verdict, not your rating, is what shifts her mood.

## 3. XP and level

Two things decide the XP on the Session Complete card:

- The **difficulty** of the session. It is fixed at 3 of 5 for every
  session, so it is the same amount every time. See
  [Difficulty Levels](./difficulty-levels.md).
- **Her verdict.** A 3 adds a little on top, a 4 adds more, a 5 adds
  the most. A 1 or a 2 adds nothing.

Her verdict is therefore the only part of the session XP you can move.

You also earn **1 XP for every message you send**, in a session or out
of one, with your own Domina and with a preset one.

Every 100 XP is one level. You reach level 2 at 100 XP, level 3 at
200 XP, and so on up to level 10, Master. Your level is shown on the
**You** screen.

The only place you set your level by hand is the result page of the
onboarding quiz, before you finish onboarding. After that it follows
your XP and there is no control that nudges it.

## 4. The summary

A two-to-four sentence summary is written by the Domina herself and
saved with the session. It is not shown anywhere in the app. It is
what the PDF export prints.

## 5. Aftercare

The End session page has a second button: **End & Start Aftercare**. It
ends the session normally, then she opens a warm closing beat in the
chat. She acknowledges what you gave her, asks how you feel, gives one
or two small grounding directives (water, breathe, blanket, rest), and
tells you to take off and clean any gear that was in play. No new
demands, no tributes, no new session offer.

After a safe word the same thing is offered as **Start Aftercare**,
under her acknowledgement. That version is pure reassurance. She does
not go over how the session went unless you raise it yourself.

## 6. Keeping and exporting

The Session Complete card stays in the conversation where it landed.
There is no session history screen. Nothing about a session is public,
and no screen in the app makes a link to one.

The card carries one action:

- **Export as PDF.** **Premium Plus only**, only for a session that
  ended normally, and only in the chat with your own Domina. A session
  you ran with a preset Domina has no export link. See
  [Session PDF Export](../features/session-pdf-export.md).

There is no button that deletes a single session. Finished sessions are
removed automatically 365 days after they started. Photos you sent and
images she made stay in your gallery and are not touched by that.
Delete those from the gallery yourself.

## What if the session ended unusually

| End state | What you see |
|---|---|
| Completed normally | Rating, her verdict, XP, summary, PDF export on Premium Plus. |
| Safeworded | A short acknowledgement from her in the chat, with aftercare offered. No XP, no verdict, no summary. |
| Left alone too long | A **Session closed** notice the next time you open the chat, telling you how long the session had been idle. No XP, no verdict, no summary. |

Neither of the last two can be rated afterwards, neither awards XP, and
neither can be exported as a PDF. Both still count against your free
daily session. A session you walked away from does not make her think
worse of you. Her mood drifts back toward neutral on its own while you
are away.

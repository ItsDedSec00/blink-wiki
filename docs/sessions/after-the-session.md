# After the Session

When you reach the outro card and confirm the end, MISTRIX asks for a
rating, your Domina passes her own verdict on the session, XP is
awarded, a summary is written, and the session is stored in your
history.

## 1. The rating

The **End session** page carries a **1 to 5** rating before you confirm
the end. It is private feedback for us. It does not move your Domina's
mood, it does not change your XP, and she never reads it.

Rate honestly anyway. It is how we find out which sessions land and
which ones do not.

## 2. Her verdict

She judges the session herself, from the transcript and nothing else.
Her verdict appears on the summary card as a score out of 5:

| Score | Label |
|---|---|
| 1 | Poor |
| 2 | Weak |
| 3 | Adequate |
| 4 | Good |
| 5 | Exemplary |

Underneath it she leaves one line addressed to you about how you
behaved. This verdict, not your rating, is what shifts her mood.

## 3. XP and level

Two things decide the XP on the summary card:

- The **difficulty** of the session. A harder session is worth more.
- **Her verdict.** A verdict of 3 or better adds a bonus on top, and
  the bonus grows with the score. A 1 or a 2 adds nothing.

Every 100 XP is one level. You reach level 2 at 100 XP, level 3 at
200 XP, and so on up to level 10, Master.

You can also adjust your level manually by one step in either direction
from the Profile screen at any time, regardless of XP.

## 4. The summary

A two-to-four sentence summary is written by the Domina herself and
saved with the session. It is the version of the session that appears
in your history.

The summary never contains:

- The full task text.
- Your interests, hard limits, or inventory.
- Any directly identifying details.

## 5. Keeping or removing a session

A session stays in your history until you remove it. Nothing about it
is public, and there is no way to publish one.

- **Download a PDF** of the session. **Premium Plus only.** See
  [Session PDF Export](../features/session-pdf-export.md).
- **Delete the session.** It goes from your history. Images from the
  session are not deleted with it; they stay in your gallery until you
  delete them there.

## What if the session ended unusually

| End state | What you see |
|---|---|
| Completed normally | Rating, her verdict, XP, summary, share / export options. |
| Safeworded | A short acknowledgement from her in the chat. No XP, no verdict, no summary. |
| Left alone too long | A **Session closed** notice the next time you open the chat, telling you how long the session had been idle. No XP, no verdict, no summary. |

Neither of the last two can be rated afterwards, and neither awards XP.
A session you walked away from does not make her think worse of you.
Her mood drifts back toward neutral on its own while you are away.

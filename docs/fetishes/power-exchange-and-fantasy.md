# Power Exchange & Fantasy

This is where MISTRIX's written medium shines hardest. Mind-play and
fantasy scenarios are mostly text and direction - the Domina is built
for that.

## Entries

- **Brainwashing** - Fantasy of having one's mind controlled or reprogrammed.
- **Drone / Robotization** - Fantasy of becoming an obedient drone - stripped of ego, running on protocols, serving on command.
- **Blackmail Fantasy** - Fiction of being 'owned' through compromising knowledge - purely roleplay, no real leverage ever involved.
- **Forced Crossdressing** - Being made to dress in clothing of another gender.
- **Hypnosis** - Erotic hypnosis - suggestions, triggers, and trance states.
- **Fantasy Scenarios** - Fictional or fantastical role-play settings.
- **Gender Swap** - Fantasy scenarios involving a change of gender.
- **Sex Worker Role-play** - Scenarios involving escort, prostitution, or transactional dynamics.
- **Trans Character** - Scenarios or fantasies involving a trans identity or character.
- **Financial Domination / Findom** - Arousal from paying tribute, gifting, and surrendering control over money to a dominant.
- **Gentle Domination / Gentledom** - Dominance expressed through warmth, praise, and patient control rather than harshness or cruelty.
- **Futanari** - Fantasy of futanari characters, feminine figures with both a penis and a vulva.
- **Cuckolding** - Arousal from a partner's intimacy with someone else, often carrying an element of humiliation.

## How the Domina uses this category

- These are **scene shapers**. A single entry here can colour an entire
  session - drone framing turns every other task into a protocol, a
  blackmail framing turns every task into a price.
- The Domina pairs them with her **speech style**. Hypnosis hits hardest
  when she is also poetic. Drone framing hits hardest when she is
  clipped.
- **Blackmail Fantasy** has an explicit safety frame: it is fiction. The
  Domina will not assemble fake "evidence", will not threaten real
  exposure, and will not collect identifying information for use later.

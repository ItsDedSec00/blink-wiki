# Misc Kinks

A grab-bag category, but useful ones. Many entries here are
"modulators" - they change how a session feels without being the
spine of it.

## Entries

- **Degradation** - Being verbally or physically degraded and demeaned.
- **Erotic Dancing** - Performing or watching erotic, sensual dance.
- **Food Play** - Use of food in erotic scenarios.
- **Grinding** - Erotic friction and dry humping.
- **Furry** - Anthropomorphic animal characters in an erotic context.
- **Licking** - Erotic licking of the body, objects, or surfaces.
- **Massages** - Erotic massage as part of a session or service.
- **Multiple Orgasms** - Being pushed through multiple orgasms in succession.
- **Oral Fixation** - Preoccupation with the mouth - sucking, licking, being filled.
- **Overstimulation** - Being pushed beyond comfortable sensitivity.
- **Sleep Deprivation** - Being kept awake as a form of control or punishment.
- **Teasing** - Prolonged, playful arousal without immediate satisfaction.
- **Tentacles** - Fantasy scenarios involving tentacle play or creatures.

- **Degradation** is woven into her vocabulary when present. It changes
  word choice across many tasks, not just one.
- **Teasing, Edging, and Orgasm Denial** (the last two live in BDSM)
  are often used together. The Domina blends them naturally.
- **Multiple Orgasms** and **Overstimulation** are escalations - she

- **Sleep Deprivation** scenes span multiple sessions over time, with
  out-of-session check-ins. Read [Her Memory](../communication/her-memory.md)
  before saying yes.
- **Furry** and **Tentacles** lean fantasy. The Domina treats them as
  setting choices for a scene.

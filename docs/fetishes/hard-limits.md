# Hard Limits

A hard limit is a line your Domina will never cross. It is the most
load-bearing safety feature in MISTRIX. Treat the list as the one
setting you re-check the most.

## How they work

- You set them on the **Fetish map** screen. Each fetish has a cross
  next to its hearts. Tap the cross and that fetish becomes a hard
  limit.
- Every entry is one of the fetishes from the same curated list you set
  interests on.
- The Domina has your hard limits in front of her in every session and
  every conversation, as a plain never.
- This is not a soft preference. The Domina will not propose, hint at,
  or write around an entry in this list.

## Hard limit vs interest

Both lists draw from the same curated catalogue. On the Fetish map a
fetish is one or the other, never both. Whatever else points at it, the
hard limit wins.

| In interests | In hard limits | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Yes (any level) | No | She uses it according to the interest level |
| No | No | Ignored |
| Either | Yes | **Never used**, regardless of interest level |

If you want to fully retire a fetish, you can either drop its interest
to 0 or make it a hard limit. Hard limits are stronger - they survive
later edits to the rest of your interests, and she says no out loud if
you ask her for one anyway.

## Adding and removing

- **Adding** a hard limit takes effect immediately. Active sessions
  continue with their existing plan, but the next session will respect
  the new limit.
- **Removing** a hard limit also takes effect immediately. The next
  session is free to use it (still gated by the interest level you set
  separately).

## "Pretend it isn't on my list tonight"

This does not work. By design. The Domina is not allowed to be
negotiated out of a hard limit by an in-chat message. If you genuinely
want a hard limit gone, clear the cross on the Fetish map screen and
start a new session.

## What is on the list by default

Nothing. You set the list during onboarding. If you skipped quickly,
revisit the screen now. A few minutes of curation here saves you a lot
of mood-killing surprises later.

## A common starting list

Many users keep at least a few items in hard limits even if they are
not on their interest list, as a belt-and-braces safeguard. Common
defaults to consider:

- Anything involving non-consenting third parties (always blocked by the
  app, but explicit on your list anyway).
- Categories your body cannot tolerate (e.g. electric stimulation if
  you have a heart condition).
- Categories your relationship situation excludes (e.g. public
  scenarios you cannot run safely right now).

Curate. Re-curate. The list is yours.

# Fetishes

MISTRIX uses a curated fetish list. You cannot invent new ones; you mark
which of the existing ones interest you, how often you want them, and
which ones are off limits forever.

## The three rules of the fetish system

1. **You cannot add custom fetishes.** The Domina works only from
   entries on the curated list. This keeps her language consistent and
   keeps her inside the lines you set.
2. **A hard limit outranks everything else.** A cross and hearts are the
   same control, so a fetish is never both. Whatever else points at a
   crossed fetish, the cross wins. Every time. Nothing you say in chat
   can override it.
3. **She starts from what you marked, then learns.** Your ratings are
   where she begins. When a chat shows real interest in something, she
   nudges that fetish up on her own, including one you never rated. A
   crossed fetish is the exception: a hard limit can never be learned
   back. **Reset AI learning** on the Fetish map clears everything she
   has picked up and leaves your own ratings alone.

## How the rating affects planning

| Rating | What you said | What she does |
|---|---|---|
| Cross | "Never." | Never mentioned, referenced or implied. |
| Circle | "Not for me." | Treated as silence; she avoids it. |
| 1 heart | "Occasionally." | Comes up now and then. |
| 2 hearts | "Often." | A regular feature of your scenes. |
| 3 hearts | "Constantly." | She reaches for it whenever it fits. |

More hearts means more often, not harder. How hard she pushes is set by
your level and her intensity setting, not by the heart count.

You can change a rating any time. Lowering one affects future sessions,
not past ones.

## Where this lives in the app

Everything is on one screen: **Fetish map**.

- Reach it from **You > Fetish map**, from the Fetish map card on the
  Dashboard, or by putting Fetishes in your bottom bar.
- Every fetish has its own row with one control: a cross, a circle, and
  three hearts. Tap one, then **Save & Continue**.
- A row can carry a cross or hearts, never both, so a hard limit can
  never be read as an interest.
- The footer counts your interests and your hard limits as you go.

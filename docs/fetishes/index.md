# Fetishes

MISTRIX uses a curated fetish list. You cannot invent new ones; you mark
which of the existing ones interest you, at what intensity, and which
ones are off limits forever.

## The three rules of the fetish system

1. **You cannot add custom fetishes.** The Domina works only from
   entries on the curated list. This keeps her language consistent and
   keeps her inside the lines you set.
2. **Hard limits always override interest.** If something appears in
   both your interests and your hard limits, the hard limit wins. Every
   time. Nothing you say in chat can override it.
3. **Anything you didn't mark stays off the menu.** The Domina only
   plans around things you said you want, at the level you said you
   want them.

## How interest levels affect planning

| Level | What you said | What she does |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (not selected) | "Not for me right now." | Treated as silence; she avoids it. |
| 1 - Curious | "I'd try it." | Occasional, lower intensity. |
| 2 - Like | "I enjoy this." | Regular feature, normal intensity. |
| 3 - Love | "This is my thing." | Often a centrepiece. |

You can change a level any time. Lowering a level affects future
sessions, not past ones.

## Where this lives in the app

- **Fetish map** screen: every fetish has its own row. Tap the hearts to
  set the interest level, then save.
- Hard limits live on the same row. Tap the cross instead of the hearts.
  A row can carry a cross or hearts, never both, so a hard limit can
  never be read as an interest.

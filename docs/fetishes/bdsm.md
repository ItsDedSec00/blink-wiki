- BDSM entries are often the **spine** of a session. Edging, chastity,

  not own.
- **Free Use** and **Public Humiliation** entries always come with a
  privacy and safety disclaimer; she does not push public scenarios on
  users at low levels.
- Multi-Domina structures (Daddy Kink, Pet Play) shift her **voice**,
  not the medium - she still writes directives.

## Safety notes

  IRL safety brief that the Domina cannot give you. Read a real-world
  primer before trying it.

  intervals.
- **Public scenarios** carry legal risk in many jurisdictions. The
  Domina assumes you know what is legal where you live.

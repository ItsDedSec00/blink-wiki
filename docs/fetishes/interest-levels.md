# Interest Levels

Every fetish carries a rating. You set it on the **Fetish map** screen,
one row per fetish, one control per row. The Domina plans around
anything rated at one heart or more.

## The rating scale

| Control | Label on screen | What it tells her |
|---|---|---|
| Cross | Hard limit | Never mention, reference or imply this. |
| Circle | Not for me | Treat this as silence. Avoid it. |
| 1 heart | Occasionally | Weave it in now and then. |
| 2 hearts | Often | Make it a regular feature. |
| 3 hearts | Constantly | Reach for this whenever it fits. |

It is a single control, so a fetish sits at exactly one of these. Tap
the cross and the hearts clear. Tap a heart and the cross clears.

## Hearts are how often, not how hard

This is the one thing worth getting right, and the screen says it too.
More hearts means she weaves the fetish into your scenes **more often**.
It does not make her rougher. How hard she pushes comes from your level
and from her own intensity setting.

The rating is also about what *you* do in your sessions, not what you
like looking at. Rating Latex at three hearts tells her you wear latex.

## How she uses the rating

- **Frequency.** Higher-rated entries come up more often.
- **Centrality.** A three-heart fetish can be the spine of a session. A
  one-heart entry is more likely to be a single directive.
- **Rotation.** Anything she has worked recently drops down the order,
  so she does not run the same theme back to back.

## Changing a rating

You can change a rating at any time from the Fetish map screen.

- Lowering a rating affects **future** sessions only. A session in
  progress still uses what it was planned with.
- Setting the circle takes the fetish off her list.
- Raising a rating makes the fetish more likely in the next plan.

## What she learns on her own

Your ratings are not the whole picture. When a chat shows real interest
or real disinterest, the Domina records a small adjustment against that
fetish, between -1 and +1, and plans on your rating plus that
adjustment. It is how she picks up on things you never got round to
rating: a fetish you left at the circle can come up on its own once she
has heard enough.

You can see the current adjustment in the chat menu under **Fetishes**.
The dots are your Fetish map rating, the green or crimson plus or minus
is what she has learned, and the Heat bar is the weight she is actually
planning on.

If it has drifted somewhere you did not intend, tap **Reset AI
learning** at the top right of the Fetish map. It asks you to confirm,
then clears every automatic adjustment. Your hearts and crosses stay
exactly as you set them.

## One heart is a good starting place

If you are new to MISTRIX or unsure about something, give it one heart.
The Domina brings it in occasionally, you can rate the session, and you
can raise or lower it after seeing how it landed.

## Hard limits beat everything

A crossed fetish is never used, and it cannot be learned back: the
adjustment she can apply is not enough to lift a cross out of the never
pile. See [Hard Limits](./hard-limits.md).

# Interest Levels

Every fetish has an interest level. There are four of them. The Domina
only proposes things you marked at level 1 or higher.

## The four levels

| Level | Label | What it tells her |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Not selected | Treat this as silence. Avoid it. |
| 1 | Curious | I would try it. Use it sparingly, at lower intensity. |
| 2 | Like | I enjoy this. Use it regularly. |
| 3 | Love | This is my thing. Feel free to centre sessions on it. |

## How she uses the levels

- **Frequency.** Higher levels appear more often across sessions.
- **Centrality.** A Love-level fetish can be the spine of a session. A
  Curious-level one is more likely to be a single task.
- **Intensity.** Curious is gentler. Love is at the intensity her own
  personality dictates.

## Changing a level

You can change a level at any time from the Fetish map screen.

- Lowering a level affects **future** sessions only. A session in
  progress still uses the level it was planned with.
- Lowering to 0 takes the item off her list entirely.
- Raising a level immediately makes the item more likely in the next
  plan.

## "Curious" is a great starting place

If you are new to MISTRIX or unsure about something, mark it Curious. The
Domina will introduce it at low intensity, you can rate the session, and
you can raise or lower the level after seeing how it landed.

## Hard limits beat interest levels

If a fetish is on your hard limits list, the interest level does not
matter. Hard limits win, every time. See [Hard Limits](./hard-limits.md).

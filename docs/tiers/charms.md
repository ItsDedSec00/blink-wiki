# Charms

Charms are a one-time-purchase in-app currency. You buy them in
bundles. You spend them on actions inside existing features. Buying or
spending Charms does not change your tier.

## What you can spend Charms on

- **AI Studio generations past your free allowance.** Once your daily
  free points no longer cover what you are generating, the cost comes
  off your Charm balance instead. Each slot has its own Charm cost, and
  you see it before you generate.
- **Findom charges.** When findom is enabled and your Domina demands
  payment, the cost is in Charms. If you pay, the Charms are deducted
  from your wallet.

Future Charm-spendable actions may be added; the wallet view always
shows the current options.

## Buying a bundle

- Open **Account > Buy charms**.
- Pick a bundle (sizes vary).
- The checkout page opens. Pay by card. It is a one-off payment, not a
  subscription.
- The Charms land in your wallet once the payment goes through.

Bundles are not refundable once delivered. If you bought a bundle by
mistake (wrong size, wrong account), contact support promptly - there
is no automatic refund flow.

## Seeing your balance

The Charms balance is shown:

- In the **Wallet** card at the top of **Account > Buy charms**.
- In a small badge in the AI Studio header when relevant.
- In the findom toggle area when a charge is open.

Your balance is not behind your PIN, so you can see it without
unlocking anything. Every purchase and every spend is kept as a running
history, and your [data export](../account/data-export.md) includes it.

## Spending Charms

Spending is one tap. The action that costs Charms shows the cost
before you confirm:

- AI Studio: the **Generate** button shows the Charm cost when your
  free points no longer cover it.
- Findom: the open charge sheet shows the Charm cost before you tap
  **Pay**.

If your balance is below the cost, the action button is disabled and
you are prompted to buy a bundle.

## Charm life cycle

- **No expiry.** Charms you buy stay on your account.
- **No tier link.** Charms survive tier changes; cancelling Premium
  does not consume them.
- **No transfer.** Charms cannot be sent to another user or moved to
  another account.

## A note on responsible spending

Charms are real money. The wallet is designed to make spending
visible, but it cannot stop you from buying bundles you cannot afford.
If runaway findom spending is a concern for you, leave findom off
(it is off by default). See [Findom](../features/findom.md).

# Charms

Charms are the in-app currency. You buy them in bundles, and you can
also earn them by inviting friends. You spend them on actions inside
features you already have. Buying or spending Charms does not change
your tier.

## What you can spend Charms on

- **AI Studio generations past your free allowance.** Once your daily
  free points no longer cover what you are generating, the cost comes
  off your Charm balance instead. Each slot has its own Charm cost, and
  you see it before you generate. See
  [AI Studio](../features/ai-studio.md).
- **AI Studio video.** A clip costs 10 or 20 Charms depending on which
  of the two options you pick. It is paid the same way as an image:
  free points first, then your balance.
- **The pictures she sends in chat.** When she offers one and you tap
  **Generate**, it costs 2 points, or 2 Charms once the points for the
  day are gone. The card tells you which before you confirm.
- **Findom charges.** When findom is enabled and your Domina demands
  payment, the cost is in Charms. If you pay, the Charms are deducted
  from your wallet. See [Findom](../features/findom.md).
- **More chat messages on Free.** 5 Charms adds 25 messages, or 15
  Charms buys a Day Pass, which lifts the allowance until midnight your
  local time. Both come out of your balance only; neither touches your
  daily free points. See [Chat limits on Free](./chat-limits.md).
- **Buying past a blocked app.** If you turned on the Charms route out
  of an app block in the companion app, the toll is paid in Charms. You
  set the amount yourself; it starts at 25. See
  [App blocking](../features/app-blocking.md).

## Buying a bundle

- Open **Account > Buy charms**, which opens the **Shop**.
- Pick one of the three bundles: **Starter pouch** (50 Charms),
  **Velvet purse** (150) or **Black coffer** (500). Each card shows the
  price and the price per Charm, so you can compare them.
- The checkout page opens. Pay by card. It is a one-off payment, not a
  subscription. There is a field for a promotion code if you have one.
- The Charms land in your wallet once the payment goes through.

Bundles are not refundable once delivered. If you bought a bundle by
mistake (wrong size, wrong account), contact support promptly - there
is no automatic refund flow. If a purchase is refunded or charged back,
the Charms come off your balance again.

## Seeing your balance

The Charms balance is shown:

- On the **Account** screen, under the **Buy charms** row.
- In the **Wallet** card at the top of the **Shop**.
- In the small chip in the AI Studio header.
- On the findom charge card, while a charge is open.

Every purchase and every spend is kept as a running history, and your
[data export](../account/data-export.md) includes it.

## Spending Charms

Spending is one tap. The action that costs Charms shows the cost
before you confirm:

- AI Studio: the **Generate** button shows the Charm cost when your
  free points no longer cover it.
- Chat pictures: the confirm card says whether this one is free today
  or what it costs.
- Findom: the open charge sheet shows the Charm cost before you tap
  **Pay**.
- Chat messages on Free: the two buttons in the **Free plan** panel
  carry their price.

If your balance is below the cost, the action button turns into a
prompt to buy a bundle and nothing is charged.

## Charm life cycle

- **No expiry.** Charms you buy stay on your account.
- **You can also earn them.** The **Invite & Earn** section of the Shop
  pays 10 Charms to you and 10 to your friend for each invite that
  counts. See [Referrals](./referrals.md).
- **A failed action is refunded by itself.** If a generation fails, a
  video job gives up, or a message top-up cannot be applied, the Charms
  go back to your wallet. You do not have to ask.
- **A refunded purchase is reversed.** If a bundle payment is refunded
  or charged back, the matching Charms are removed from your wallet.
- **No tier link.** Charms survive tier changes; cancelling Premium
  does not consume them.
- **No transfer.** Charms cannot be sent to another user or moved to
  another account.

## A note on responsible spending

Charms are real money. The wallet is designed to make spending
visible, but it cannot stop you from buying bundles you cannot afford.
If runaway findom spending is a concern for you, leave findom off
(it is off by default). See [Findom](../features/findom.md).

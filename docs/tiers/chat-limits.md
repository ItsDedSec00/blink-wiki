# Chat limits on Free

Two different limits can stop you mid-chat, and they look alike on
screen. This page keeps them apart.

- The **free message allowance** is a Free-plan limit. It refills over
  time, and Charms or Premium clear it.
- The **hourly and daily ceiling** is a spam guard. Every plan has one.
  Paid plans sit higher, but no plan removes it, so mostly you wait it
  out.

The card in the chat tells you which one you hit. "Free messages used
up" is the allowance. "Chat limit reached" is the ceiling.

## The free message allowance

On Free you have **30 messages** ready to send. A new account starts
with all 30. They refill over time: one comes back about every 30
minutes, back up to 30.

It is one allowance across every Domina you talk to, not 30 each.

Premium and Premium Plus have no message allowance at all. This limit
exists only on Free.

### What does not use it up

- **Every message inside a session.** Once a session is running, the
  allowance is not touched until it ends.
- **The safe word.** Always available, at any count.
- A session timer going off, and the start of aftercare.
- A reply that failed. You only pay for messages she answered.

Regenerating a reply does not use a message either, but she will not
regenerate while the allowance is empty.

### When it runs out

The chat shows a card headed **Free messages used up** with a countdown
to the next one. The message you typed is put back in the input box, so
you can send it when the clock lands.

You have four ways forward:

| Option | Cost | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Wait** | free | The clock on the card. Messages come back one by one. |
| **+25 messages** | 5 Charms | 25 messages added on the spot. |
| **Day Pass** | 15 Charms | Unlimited messages until midnight, your local time. |
| **Upgrade to Premium** | subscription | No message allowance at all. |

Both Charm options are paid from your Charm balance. If the balance is
too low, nothing is charged and you are asked to pick up a bundle
first. Messages you buy sit on top of the free 30 and stay on your
account until you send them.

### Seeing what is left

Open the **Overview** menu in the chat header. The **Free plan** panel
at the top has a **Messages** row: how many you have left, and a live
clock to the next one. The two Charm buttons sit right under it.

While a Day Pass is running, that row reads "Day Pass, unlimited until
midnight" and the buy buttons are hidden.

## The hourly and daily ceiling

This one has nothing to do with being on Free. It is a spam guard, and
every account has it.

| Plan | Messages per hour | Messages per day |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 90 | 250 |
| Premium | 120 | no daily limit |
| Premium Plus | 120 | no daily limit |

A running Day Pass counts as a paid plan here: 120 an hour and no daily
limit for as long as it lasts.

Unlike the allowance, this ceiling counts everything - messages inside
a session, and messages you paid for.

### When you hit it

The chat shows a card headed **Chat limit reached**. It says whether it
was the hourly or the daily count and counts down to when you can send
again. There are no Charm buttons on this card. On Free it points at
Premium, which raises the hourly number and drops the daily one. When
the clock ends, the chat simply carries on.

Anything you already bought is not lost. Purchased messages and a
running Day Pass are still there and send again the moment the timer
ends.

On Free you have to work at this one. The allowance alone cannot get
you near 90 messages in an hour, so it normally takes a long session or
a few Charm top-ups inside the same hour.

### Two much shorter pauses

Same family, seconds rather than hours:

- **One at a time.** Send again while she is still writing and you get
  a short notice instead of a second reply.
- **Two seconds between messages.** Tap send twice in a row and a small
  notice asks you to wait.

Neither costs you anything. Send again.

## Which card is which

| The card says | Which limit | What clears it |
|---|---|---|
| Free messages used up | The Free allowance | Waiting, Charms, or Premium |
| Chat limit reached | The hourly or daily ceiling | Waiting, or a paid plan's higher ceiling |

# Referrals

Every account has its own invite link. If someone signs up with it and
stays active, you both get 10 Charms. Reach 25 counted invites in a
quarter and you also get 20 percent off a subscription payment.

Every tier can invite. Free accounts earn Charms exactly like paying
ones. There is one discount that needs a subscription, described at the
bottom of this page.

## Where to find your link

- Open **Account > Buy charms** to reach the **Shop**.
- Scroll to **Invite & Earn**.
- Tap **Copy link**, or post it straight to Reddit or X with the two
  buttons next to it.

The link carries your personal invite code, so there is nothing to type
out. The same section shows how many invites have counted this quarter,
how many are still waiting, and how many days are left before the
counter resets.

## What counts as a verified invite

An invite pays out only after your friend has done all of this:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **A new account** | They register a brand new account after opening your link. An existing account cannot be attached to you afterwards. |
| **The same tab** | They sign up in the browser tab they opened your link in. If they close that tab before signing up, the signup is not attached to you. |
| **7 days** | Their account is at least 7 days old. |
| **One finished session** | They have completed at least one session, all the way to the summary. |

A session that ends with the safe word does not count as completed, and
neither does one that is left sitting until it is marked abandoned. See
[Lifecycle of a Session](../sessions/lifecycle.md).

## What does not count

- Inviting yourself. A second account of your own is never valid.
- Signups that the anti-abuse checks reject. What those checks look at
  is not published.
- Invites where the friend never finishes a session. 90 days after the
  signup the invite expires and can no longer pay out.

If an invite is rejected, nothing is deducted from you. It simply never
counts.

## When the Charms arrive

Payouts are not instant. Once your friend meets every requirement
above, both sides are paid:

- **Your friend gets 10 Charms.** Always, even if you are at your cap.
- **You get 10 Charms.** As long as you are under your quarterly cap.

A payout can take up to a day to land after the last requirement is
met. That is never sooner than 7 days after your friend signs up, and
later if it takes them longer to finish a session. Until then the
**Invite & Earn** section counts them as pending.

Charms go straight to your wallet. [Charms](./charms.md) covers what you
can spend them on.

## The quarterly cap

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Counted invites per quarter | 25 |
| Quarter length | 3 months, running from the day your current link was issued |
| Charms per verified invite | 10 for you, 10 for your friend |

Invites beyond 25 stop paying you Charms for the rest of that quarter.
Your friend still gets their 10 either way.

When the quarter ends you get a fresh invite link and the counter goes
back to zero. Links you shared earlier keep working, and anyone they
bring in counts toward whichever quarter is running at the time.

## Discount one: reaching the cap

The 25th counted invite in a quarter earns you 20 percent off a
subscription payment. A **Claim your 20% off** button appears in the
**Invite & Earn** section.

- If you already subscribe, it is applied to your subscription and comes
  off your next payment.
- If you are on Free, it is saved and comes off by itself at your next
  Premium or Premium Plus checkout. The section then reads "20% saved
  for your next checkout".

It is one-off: 20 percent off a single payment, not a permanent price
change. It does not apply to Charm bundles. One per quarter, and one at
a time: until a discount you already earned has come off a payment,
reaching the cap again does not add another.

## Discount two: your friend subscribes

If a friend whose invite was verified goes on to subscribe, both of you
can get 20 percent off a payment. This works for any verified invite,
including ones past your quarterly cap:

| Who | What they get | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Your friend** | 20 percent off one payment | They hold an active Premium or Premium Plus subscription. Once per invite. |
| **You** | 20 percent off one payment | You are on Premium or Premium Plus yourself. Once per quarter. |

This is the only part of the program that depends on your tier. On Free
you still earn Charms and can still reach the cap, but this second
discount needs an active subscription on your side. The section shows a
state next to it: **Ready** when it is available, **Claimed** when you
have used it this quarter, and **Premium** when your tier does not
qualify yet.

Both discounts are applied for you. There is no code to enter at
checkout.

# Upgrading, Downgrading, and Cancelling

Everything to do with your plan sits in one place: the
**Subscription** row under **Plan** in **Account**. It shows the plan
you are on now.

| Your plan | What the row opens |
|---|---|
| Free | The plan chooser, with both paid plans side by side. |
| Premium or Premium Plus | The subscription screen: change plan, change your card, see past invoices, cancel. |

Payment runs on a separate checkout page. Card details are typed there
and never inside MISTRIX.

## Upgrading from Free

- Open **Account > Plan > Subscription**.
- Pick **Premium** (€15 a month) or **Premium Plus** (€25 a month).
- The checkout page opens. It has a field for a promotion code, unless
  a [referral](./referrals.md) reward is already being applied to this
  checkout. In that case the discount is on it from the start and there
  is no code field.
- Pay.
- You come back to a short confirmation screen. Your new plan is
  normally live a second or two later.

What you keep:

- Your sessions, history, level, XP, and Charm balance.
- Your fetish interests, hard limits, inventory, and PIN-protected
  data.
- Your chosen preset Domina.

What changes:

- The daily session cap is gone. Free is one session per 24 hours.
- The free message allowance is gone. Nothing to wait for and nothing
  to top up. The hourly spam guard stays, at 120 messages an hour, and
  the daily one drops away. See [Chat limits on Free](./chat-limits.md).
- You can create a personal Domina: one on Premium, up to three on
  Premium Plus.
- More [chat models](../communication/model-selector.md) become
  selectable. **Classic** is on every plan; **Quicksilver** and
  **Velvet** need a paid plan.
- Your daily free AI Studio points go from 3 to 10, or to 30 on
  Premium Plus.
- Character shots for your personal Domina become available.
- You can hold 10 active routines instead of 4 (20 on Premium Plus),
  she can mark a routine done for you, and routine history opens up
  from this week to the last four.
- Open tasks stop being capped at one at a time.
- She can freeze a chastity timer, and **High** notification frequency
  becomes selectable.

## Upgrading from Premium to Premium Plus

Same row, but it opens the subscription screen rather than a fresh
checkout page. You see the exact amount and a confirm step before
anything is charged. The new plan applies as soon as the change goes
through. You do not have to do anything else.

What you keep: everything, including your existing personal Domina.

What changes: you can create up to two more personal Dominas, your
daily free AI Studio points go to 30, and your active-routine cap goes
from 10 to 20.

## Downgrading from Premium Plus to Premium

Done from the subscription screen, with the same confirm step. The
Premium limits below apply from the moment the change goes through.

What you keep: every personal Domina you have made. None of them are
deleted, they stay in your list, and you can still switch which one is
active.

What changes:

- Premium allows one personal Domina, so creating a new one stays
  blocked while you still hold the ones you made on Premium Plus.
- Your daily free AI Studio points go back to 10.
- Your active-routine cap goes back to 10. Routines over that number
  are not removed; you just cannot add another until you are under it.

## Downgrading to Free

There is no separate "move me to Free" button. Going back to Free
means cancelling, which is covered in the next section. The change
takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, not
the moment you confirm.

What you keep: your account, chat history, session history, level, XP,
Charms, fetish interests, hard limits, and everything you made in AI
Studio.

What changes:

- You go back to one session per 24 hours.
- The free message allowance comes back, and with it the lower ceiling
  of 90 messages an hour and 250 a day. See
  [Chat limits on Free](./chat-limits.md).
- Your chat model goes back to **Classic**. Your old pick is
  remembered and comes back if you subscribe again.
- Your daily free AI Studio points drop to 3. You still generate, and
  she still sends pictures in chat; you just get less of it for free.
- The public gallery shows you the 30 newest posts instead of all of
  them.
- Your personal Dominas are still on your account, but Free chats with
  a preset Domina instead. The app asks you to pick one if you have
  not already.
- Active routines are capped at 4, she stops marking them done for
  you, and routine history shows the current week only.
- You hold one open task at a time.
- She can no longer freeze a chastity timer. Unfreezing is never
  gated, so a timer she froze while you paid can still be released.
- **High** notification frequency drops back to **Normal**. Your
  choice is remembered for the next time you subscribe.

## Cancelling

Cancel from **Account > Plan > Subscription**. Cancelling does not cut
your access off there and then. It schedules the subscription to stop
at the end of the period you have already paid for, and you keep your
plan in full until that date.

### After you cancel

The next time you open the home screen, a card headed **Before you go**
appears. There is no countdown on it and it does not block anything.
It tells you:

- The date your current plan runs until.
- That your next invoice is 20 percent off if you decide to stay. The
  discount is offered once per subscription, so it will not be there
  if you have taken it before, or if your subscription already carries
  a discount such as a [referral](./referrals.md) reward. In that case
  the card simply offers to continue without one.

You have two choices on the card:

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Stay Premium** / **Stay Premium Plus** | The cancellation is undone straight away and your subscription carries on. If the discount was on offer, it is applied to your next invoice. |
| **Keep my cancellation** | The card disappears and your cancellation stands. |

**Keep my cancellation** hides the card for good on that device. It
will not come back, on that device, even if you cancel again later.
Your access is untouched either way, and you still keep your plan
until the date shown.

Once that date passes you become Free. You can subscribe again at any
time, and your personal Dominas are still there.

## What happens to your Charms

Nothing. Charms are separate from your plan.

- A tier change never adds, removes, or freezes Charms.
- Charms do not expire.
- You can still spend them on Free, for example on AI Studio
  generations past your daily allowance.

See [Charms](./charms.md).

## What happens to what you made

Nothing you made on a paid plan is deleted when your plan changes.

- AI Studio images and videos remain in your own gallery, whatever
  plan you are on.
- Personal Dominas are never deleted by a plan change. Their names,
  settings, portraits, and history stay put.
- Finished sessions and their summaries are not deleted by a plan
  change.

A plan change limits what you can make and use next. It does not take
away what you have already made.

## If a payment fails

A failed renewal does not drop you to Free on the spot. Your plan
keeps working while the payment is retried. If it never goes through,
the subscription ends and you become Free.

Update your card from **Account > Plan > Subscription**.

## Refunds

MISTRIX does not issue automatic refunds. For specific cases such as an
accidental double payment or a cancellation right after a first
charge, contact support. If a charm purchase is refunded or charged
back, those Charms come off your balance again.

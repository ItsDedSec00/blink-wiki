# AI Studio

AI Studio is MISTRIX's standalone image and video generator. It works
for every signed-in user, on every tier. Prompts you write here do not
affect your Domina. Pictures she sends you in chat do land in your
studio gallery, labelled **Chat**, and they draw on the same daily
allowance as the studio.

## What you can do

- Generate images from a text prompt.
- Pick one of three image slots, each with its own look.
- Guide a generation with a reference image: one you upload, a Domina
  portrait, or **Remix** of an image you already have.
- Animate a reference image into a short video clip.
- **Enhance & Save** any generation to your private gallery.
- Share single images and clips to the public gallery, anonymously.

## The three image slots

| Slot | Cost once your free points are gone | Reference image |
|---|---|---|
| **Turbo** | 2 Charms | not supported |
| **Uncensored** | 3 Charms | supported |
| **NSFW** | 4 Charms | not supported |

Each slot has its own visual signature. Try the same prompt on all
three to see which one fits the look you want. The cost is printed on
the slot chip before you generate.

As soon as you pick a reference image, the slots that cannot use one
grey out and the studio moves you to a slot that can.

Prompts are capped at 500 characters. The counter in the corner of the
prompt box shows how many you have used.

## Your daily allowance

Every generation costs points, and the price in points is the same
number as the price in Charms. You get a free pot of points each day,
counted down by the **free today** badge above the prompt box. Charms
are only touched once that pot can no longer cover what you are
generating.

| Tier | Free points per day |
|---|---|
| Free | 3 |
| Premium | 10 |
| Premium Plus | 30 |

Payment is all or nothing. If the full cost fits in what is left of
today's pot, the generation is free. If it does not fit, the whole
cost comes off your Charm balance instead; the pot is never spent half
way. The **Generate** button says which of the two will happen before
you tap it.

The same pot pays for the pictures your Domina sends in chat, at 2
points each. It resets at midnight UTC. See
[Charms](../tiers/charms.md).

## Enhance & Save

After a successful generation you can tap **Enhance & Save**. This
makes a sharper, larger version of the image and saves it to your
private gallery. It can take a minute or two.

- Enhance & Save is **always free**. It costs no points and no Charms,
  and you get one enhance per successful generation.
- To keep a copy on your device, open the image in your gallery and
  tap **Download**.
- **↻ Regenerate** runs your prompt again and does cost another
  generation.

## Using a reference image

Three sources, all of them work the same way:

- **Upload** - pick an image from your device. A large photo is fine.
  The upload is kept for one hour and then deleted, so upload it again
  if you come back later.
- **A Domina portrait** - the round portraits in the reference row are
  your own Dominas. Useful for scenes with her in them.
- **Remix** - open any image in your gallery or in the public gallery
  and tap **✨ Remix** to use it as the reference.

Leave all three unset and you are on the **Text** tile, which is a
plain text-to-image generation.

## Video

Switch the composer from **Image** to **Video**. Video always animates
a still image, so you have to pick a reference first: an upload, a
Domina portrait, or a remix. That image becomes the first frame, and
your prompt describes the motion.

| Option | Cost | Clip |
|---|---|---|
| **NSFW Model** | 10 Charms | 5 seconds, 720p |
| **HQ Uncensored** | 20 Charms | 5 seconds, 720p |

A clip is paid for the same way as an image: out of today's free
points first, out of your Charm balance when they no longer cover it.

- A clip takes a few minutes. You can leave the screen or close the
  app; it keeps generating and appears under **Videos** when it is
  done.
- You can have **3 clips generating at once**. Start a fourth and you
  are asked to wait for one to finish.
- If a clip fails, the card tells you and the charge is put back. Tap
  the ✕ on the card to clear it.
- Open a finished clip to **Download** it, **Share publicly**, or
  delete it.
- To animate something you already made, open it and tap **🎬 Video
  Remix**.

## How long things are kept

| What | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Images | 365 days |
| Video clips | 90 days |
| Failed video attempts | 7 days |

This applies to shared items too. Download anything you want to keep
for good.

## What stays private

- Studio prompts are not covered by your PIN encryption. They are kept
  so your gallery can show you what you asked for.
- Images and clips you save are visible only to you until you share
  them.
- Reference uploads are deleted after one hour, and the image or clip
  you generated from one does **not** keep it attached.

To share an image or a clip, see [Public Gallery](./public-gallery.md).

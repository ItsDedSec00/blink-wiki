# Routines and tasks

A routine is something you do again and again on a schedule. A task is
something you do once and finish. Both live on the same screen, and both
end up in front of your Domina: she can see what is due, what you did,
and what you skipped.

Open **Routines** from the bottom bar, or tap the **Routines** tile on
the home screen.

## What the screen shows

From top to bottom: a **Chastity** card, then **Tasks** (your open
one-time tasks), then **Today's tasks** (routines due today), then
**Future tasks** (routines scheduled for later this week, routines you
have already met for the week, and routines you suspended). **Add
routine** sits at the bottom.

The two routine groups are labelled "tasks" in the app, but they only
ever hold routines. Your one-time tasks stay in the Tasks section at the
top.

## Setting a routine

Tap **Add routine**.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Short name, up to 80 characters. |
| **Instruction** | What you actually do, up to 400 characters. |
| **How often** | **Specific weekdays** (pick any days) or **Times per week** (1 to 7). |
| **Time of day** | Anytime, Morning, Afternoon, Evening or Night. |
| **Completion** | **I mark it done**, or **Only my Domina** (Premium). |

Time of day is a soft window, not a deadline. She will not press you on
an evening routine at nine in the morning.

You can also have her design one. In chat, open the **Routines & Tasks**
panel and tap **Have her set a new routine**. She writes the title, the
instruction and the schedule based on what she knows about you and what
you have just been talking about. It arrives as a proposal, and nothing
is added to your list until you accept it.

## How many you can hold

| | Free | Premium | Premium Plus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active routines | 4 | 10 | 20 |
| Open tasks at once | 1 | no limit | no limit |
| She marks it done | no | yes | yes |
| Adherence history | this week | last 4 weeks | last 4 weeks |

Hitting the routine limit never removes anything. It only stops you
adding another until one is gone.

## Marking a routine done

A self-check routine has a square on its row. Tap it to mark today done,
tap it again the same day to undo.

A routine set to **Only my Domina** has no tick you can use. Open the
row and tap **Tell your Domina you did it**. That opens her chat with
the routine attached, and she is the one who marks it complete. If she
does not accept your report, it stays undone.

If a routine or task was set inside a preset Domina's chat, her name
shows on the row, and reports about it go to her chat rather than to
your main Domina.

## Suspending a week

Open a routine and tap **Suspend this week**. Nothing counts as missed
for the rest of that week, and the days show as excused. **Resume this
week** puts it back. This works on every tier and does not need her
permission, but she is told that you suspended it.

## Your history

Open a routine to see a grid of dots, one per day. A filled dot is a day
you did it, a gold ring is due today, a red ring is a day you missed, a
plain ring is a day still to come, a dashed ring is a suspended week,
and a faint dot is a day the routine was not scheduled. Free shows the
current week only. Premium and Premium Plus show the last four weeks.

## Why you cannot edit or delete a routine

A routine locks the moment it is created. There is no edit button and no
delete button anywhere on the screen. Both paths go through her:

- **Request change** - you write what you want changed and why, up to
  300 characters. If she approves, you get exactly one edit and the row
  shows **Edit routine - she approved**. Save your change and the
  routine locks again.
- **Request removal** - you write your reason. If she approves, the
  routine leaves your list. If she refuses, it stays exactly as it was.

She answers in chat, in character, and she can say no to either. While a
request is open, the routine shows it as pending until she rules on it.

## One-time tasks

Tap **Add** next to the Tasks heading.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Short name, up to 80 characters. |
| **Instruction** | What you have to do, up to 400 characters. |
| **Deadline** | No deadline, Tonight, Tomorrow, In 3 days, or This week. |
| **Completion** | **I mark it done**, or **She confirms it** (Premium). |

The deadline is soft. It changes when your reminder arrives and when the
task starts reading as overdue. Nothing happens on its own when it
passes, and the task does not expire.

The chat panel has **Have her set a task** as well, and it works like
the routine version: she designs it, you accept it.

Free members hold one open task at a time and have to finish or be
released from it before a new one can be set. Premium and Premium Plus
have no limit.

To finish a self-check task, tap the circle on its row, or open it and
tap **Mark done**. A task set to **She confirms it** is reported in chat
with **Tell your Domina you did it**, and only she closes it.

To get out of a task, tap **Drop** on the row. That is not a delete: it
opens her chat and tells her you want out, and she rules on it. She may
refuse, and she may not take the request kindly. Until she grants it,
the task stays on your list. Finished tasks drop off the list on their
own.

## When you miss one

For each active routine she is shown how many times you have done it
this week against its target, which days you did it, which days you
missed, and whether you suspended the week. For each open task she is
shown its deadline and whether it is overdue.

Then she brings it up. That is all that happens. A missed routine is not
deleted, costs no [Charms](../tiers/charms.md), locks nothing, and
triggers no automatic penalty. The only thing that changes is what she
has to say to you.

Two things she is told not to get wrong:

- A "times per week" routine is not late until the week runs out. Any
  remaining day still counts.
- A routine with a time of day is not late before that part of the day
  arrives.

A week you suspended is recorded as your choice, not as a miss.

## Reminders

If you have notifications turned on, each routine that is still due gets
one reminder that day, timed to its own time of day. A routine set to
Anytime is reminded somewhere between 9am and 10pm. If its time of day
has already passed, the reminder still comes, just later. Nothing
arrives after 10pm, except a routine set to Night, which can remind you
up to midnight. A routine you have already marked done sends nothing. A
task reminds you shortly before its deadline, and a task with no
deadline sends no reminder at all.

## What she can read

The title and the instruction of every routine and task are encrypted on
your device with a key derived from your PIN. Your Domina is shown the
title, the schedule and your completion record, because that is what she
needs to hold you to it. If your PIN is locked, the screen asks for it
before it can show anything.

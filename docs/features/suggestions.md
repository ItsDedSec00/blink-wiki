# Suggestions

Suggestions is a shared list of ideas for MISTRIX. You can post your
own and vote on everyone else's. It is open to every account, on Free,
Premium and Premium Plus, and it costs no Charms.

## Getting there

- Open **Account**.
- Scroll to **Community & About**.
- Tap **Suggestions**.

The screen is titled **Feature Suggestions**.

## Writing a suggestion

Tap **+ Submit a suggestion**. A short form opens. While it is open
that button reads **Cancel**, and tapping it closes the form again.

| Field | What to put in it |
|---|---|
| **Category** | **Feature**, **UI/UX**, **Bug** or **General**. General is picked for you. |
| **Title** | Required. Up to 120 characters. One line saying what you want. |
| **Description** | Required. Up to 800 characters. What you want and why. |

Tap **Submit**. You get a "Suggestion submitted. Thank you!" message
and your entry appears at the top of the list straight away.

Two things worth knowing before you tap Submit:

- You cannot edit or delete a suggestion afterwards. Read it once
  first.
- The list does not show who wrote what, but everyone can read the
  text. Keep personal details, real names and contact details out of
  it.

If something is broken and you need an answer, this is the wrong
place. Use **Help & support** in Account instead.

## Voting

Every entry has an arrow and a number on its left.

- Tap the arrow to vote. The arrow and the count turn gold.
- Tap it again to take your vote back.
- One vote per suggestion. You can vote on as many different
  suggestions as you like, including your own.

The list is ordered by votes, highest first. Entries on the same
number of votes are ordered newest first, so a fresh idea is not
buried under old ones with the same score.

## Reading the list

- The grey tag next to a title is the category it was posted under.
- Long descriptions are cut to two lines. Tap **Show more** to read
  the rest and **Show less** to fold it back.
- Under each description is a status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Posted and collecting votes. Nothing decided. |
| **Planned** | On the list of things to build. No date attached. |
| **Done** | Built and shipped. |

## What happens after you post

Statuses are set by the MISTRIX team, not by the vote count. Votes are
a signal about what people want, nothing more.

Nothing on this list is a promise. A suggestion with a lot of votes
may never be built, and one with no votes at all may still get built.
No status, and no number of votes, means work has started.

Entries do not stay on the list forever:

- A suggestion marked **Done** stays visible for about two weeks after
  that, then drops off.
- A suggestion that is closed without being built stops appearing on
  the list straight away.

Either way, your votes on it disappear with it. Nothing else on your
account changes.

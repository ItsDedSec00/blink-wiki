# Features

Beyond chat and sessions, MISTRIX ships extra features that build on the
same Domina, fetish, and inventory data. Each one is optional. Each one
respects the same safety rules.

## Pages in this section

- [AI Studio](./ai-studio.md) - standalone image and video generation with three image slots and a private gallery.
- [Public gallery](./public-gallery.md) - sharing AI Studio images and clips anonymously to the community feed.
- [Routines and tasks](./routines-and-tasks.md) - the recurring routines and one-time tasks she sets for you.
- [Chastity](./chastity.md) - handing a real Chaster lock to your Domina, and how to get out again.
- [App blocking](./app-blocking.md) - putting apps on your phone out of reach, and the ways back in.
- [Expose](./expose.md) - the opt-in public board she can publish to.
- [Findom](./findom.md) - opt-in financial-domination flow with Charm-wallet hard gating.
- [Notifications](./notifications.md) - messages from her when the app is closed, and how often they come.
- [Mistrix Companion](./mistrix-bridge.md) - the optional Android app, and the six permissions it asks for.
- [Suggestions](./suggestions.md) - post an idea for MISTRIX and vote on other people's.

## Where to find them in the app

| Feature | Screen | Required tier |
|---|---|---|
| AI Studio | **AI Studio** from the dashboard | any signed-in user |
| Public gallery | inside AI Studio | any signed-in user (Free sees 30 newest) |
| Routines and tasks | **Routines** in the bottom bar or the sidebar | any tier; how many you can hold differs |
| Chastity | **Features > Chastity** | any tier; needs your own Chaster account |
| App blocking | **Account > This phone**, the App blocking switch | any tier; that section only exists inside the companion app |
| Expose | **Features > Expose** for the settings; the board is **Expose** in the bottom bar | any tier; off until you turn it on |
| Findom | **Features > Findom** | any tier; off until you turn it on |
| Notifications | **Account > Notifications** | any tier; the High setting needs a paid plan |
| Mistrix Companion | **Account > Android app** | any tier; Android only, and not on the Play Store |
| Suggestions | **Account > Suggestions** | any signed-in user |

## Quick reference

- **AI Studio** gives you a pot of free points each day, shared with the
  portraits she sends you in chat. What one generation costs in points
  depends on the slot you pick. When the pot no longer covers it, the
  cost comes off your Charm balance instead. See
  [AI Studio](./ai-studio.md) for the numbers.
- **Enhance & Save** in AI Studio is always free.
- **Findom** never charges real money on its own. Real money only moves
  if you complete a Charm purchase from the bundle list.
- **Chastity and app blocking** reach into the physical world. Both have
  an exit that does not depend on your Domina agreeing. Read those two
  pages before you turn either on.
- **An image or clip you post to the public gallery from AI Studio
  leaves MISTRIX.** It gets a link anyone can open, with no account.
  The Expose board is different: it is public to members, but it sits
  behind a sign-in. See [Public gallery](./public-gallery.md).

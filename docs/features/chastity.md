# Chastity

Chastity connects a real chastity lock to MISTRIX. You link your own
Chaster account, hand a lock over, and your Domina becomes its
keyholder: she can add time, take time off, pause the clock, hide it
from you, or let you out early. You decide in advance which of those
she may do.

This is the only feature that reaches a physical device. Read
[Getting out](#getting-out) before you start, not after.

**MISTRIX can never perform an emergency opening.** Keep a physical
emergency key, bolt cutters, or a backup combination reachable at all
times. Your physical safety is your responsibility.

## Connecting

You need a Chaster account. Chaster is a separate service run by
somebody else, and you sign up for it yourself at chaster.app.

1. Open **Features**, then **Chastity**.
2. Tap **Connect Chaster.app** and sign in to Chaster.
3. You land back in MISTRIX and the card shows the account you signed
   in as.

Connecting on its own does nothing to any lock. **Chastity tracking**,
the master switch, is on from that moment: every Domina you chat with
can then read your lock and use the permissions below. Turning that
switch off is not your decision. Only your primary Domina can, and you
ask her in chat. The one exception is having no primary Domina at all.
Then there is nobody to ask, and the switch is yours to turn off.
Disconnecting Chaster is a different thing, and that one is always
yours.

## Bringing a lock in

With Chaster connected, tracking on, and no lock running yet, you get
two buttons.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start a new chastity lock** | Creates a fresh lock on Chaster with MISTRIX as keyholder. You never see the code that opens it. |
| **Use an existing lock** | Lists the locks you already wear so you can hand one over or link it. |

In the picker each lock offers exactly one action:

- **Hand over**, for a lock that accepts keyholder offers. MISTRIX
  becomes its keyholder and controls your time and your release.
- **Link read-only**, for anything else (a private lock, one somebody
  else keyholds, one created from a shared lock). Your Domina sees the
  lock and its status and can weave it into your sessions, but cannot
  change it. Once the lock is linked, an **Unlink** button sits on its
  card, and it is never gated.

Starting a lock opens a consent dialog you have to confirm. The picker
spells out the same warning before you tap **Hand over**. Either way the
trust you grant is **permanent**. Chaster does not let you take it back,
and you cannot open the lock yourself before the timer ends.

Chaster can refuse, and MISTRIX passes its reason on to you. The two
common ones:

- A free Chaster account allows one lock at a time, and a finished lock
  that was never archived still counts. Archive the old one on Chaster,
  then come back.
- Both ways of giving her the key go through Chaster's community
  features, which stay closed until your Chaster account is verified.
  Starting a lock here creates it from a shared lock, and handing over a
  lock you already wear sends a keyholder offer. Verify your account in
  your Chaster account settings. Until then you can still link a lock
  read-only.

## What she may do

Tap **Settings** on the Chastity screen to open the permission panel.
Everything is off by default except adding time, and you can change any
of it whenever you like.

| Permission | What she can do with it | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Min time per adjustment** | Smallest change she may make in one go. | none |
| **Max time per adjustment** | Largest change she may make in one go. Nothing above 7 days is possible. | 1h |
| **Allow add time** | Put time on the clock, within the bounds above. | On |
| **Allow remove time** | Take time off the clock, within the same bounds. | Off |
| **Allow freeze / unfreeze** | Pause the countdown and start it again when she decides. Premium. | Off |
| **Allow early release** | Open the lock before the timer runs out. | Off |
| **Allow hide timer** | Hide the remaining time so you serve blind. | Off |
| **Allow Pillory** | Put you in Chaster's public pillory. | Off |
| **Allow Hygiene Break** | Grant you a temporary opening. | Off |

Freezing is the one permission that needs **Premium** or **Premium
Plus**. Every other permission works on the Free tier. Unfreezing is
never gated: if she froze your timer while you were Premium and your
plan later lapses, she can still start it again.

### Time changes

She adds or removes time in chat, in character, as part of a scene.
Whatever she reaches for is forced into the range you set. If she
wanted more than your maximum, your maximum is what lands. If she named
less than your minimum, your minimum lands instead. Either way a short
note under her message tells you so.

### Pillory and hygiene break

The pillory puts a line about you on Chaster's public feed, where any
Chaster member can read it. Your Domina writes that line herself, and
it always opens with the same fixed sentence naming MISTRIX. A pillory
runs between 5 minutes and 24 hours. A hygiene break is a temporary
opening, submitted on your behalf.

Both rely on an option on the Chaster lock. Switching the permission on
adds it, switching the permission off removes it again. The small label
beside each switch tells you where you stand: **Available**,
**Cooldown** (Chaster will allow the next one later) or **Attaches on
first use**.

### Hidden timer

While the timer is hidden you see "Hidden by your Domina" instead of a
countdown, and the amounts in your history are blanked out too, so the
history cannot give away what the lock hides. Ask her to show it again.

## Where you see your lock

**Features > Chastity** has the full card: status, locked since,
unlocks in, keyholder, and any of the labels Frozen, Read-only, Trusted
and Timer hidden. **Routines** shows a compact Chastity tile at the
top, and **Features > Feature history** lists everything she has done
with the lock over the last 30 days, newest first.

## Release and extension

While Chastity is on, release is hers to give. In chat you can ask her
to:

- **Let you out.** If **Allow early release** is on and she agrees, the
  lock opens there and then.
- **Pause Chastity.** She hands your lock back first, so it becomes a
  self-lock again, and then switches the feature off. The lock keeps
  running. Your Chaster account stays connected, so turning the feature
  back on later needs no new sign-in.
- **Remove Chastity entirely.** That disconnects Chaster as well.

She can also say no. That is the arrangement you opted into, which is
exactly why the exit below does not go through her.

## Getting out

**Disconnect Chaster** sits at the bottom of the Chastity screen. It is
never gated, never blocked, and never needs her permission. What it
costs you:

| What happens | Detail |
|---|---|
| Your lock is handed back | MISTRIX stops being the keyholder. The lock becomes a self-lock. |
| Your settings are reset | Permissions and bounds go back to the defaults. |
| Your history is deleted | The record of what she did with the lock is gone. |
| Your device is not opened | Disconnecting is not an unlock. |

You can reconnect at any time and start over from the defaults.

There is also **Hand my lock back**, which resigns the keyholding
without disconnecting anything. It appears on the Chastity screen when
MISTRIX still holds your key and there is nobody to ask, that is, when
Chastity tracking is already off or you have no primary Domina. MISTRIX
also offers it to you directly after you use your
[safe word](../safety/safe-word.md).

If Chaster refuses to hand the lock back, nothing is disconnected and
MISTRIX says so plainly rather than telling you that you are free when
you are not. The connection stays intact and you can try again.

### What disconnecting does not do

Disconnecting ends MISTRIX's part in this. It does not end the lock.
Your lock keeps running as a self-lock with the time still on it, and
from that point it is between you and Chaster. The only exit Chaster
always allows is giving up the lock there and waiting out the recovery
period it imposes for that.

This is why the emergency key matters. Do not start a lock you are not
prepared to sit in.

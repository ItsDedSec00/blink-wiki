# Session PDF Export

Premium Plus users can download a completed session as a PDF. It is a
one-page record of the session, not a transcript of it.

## How to download

The button sits on the **Session Complete** card, the one that appears
in your chat when a session finishes.

1. Scroll back to that card in the chat.
2. Tap **Export as PDF**.
3. Save the file.

There is no limit on how many PDFs you can make.

## What is included

- Session **title** and **completion date**.
- Your **Domina's name**.
- The **difficulty**.
- The **AI-written summary** of the session.
- Your **rating** and the optional **note** you left.
- An **XP badge** for the session.
- MISTRIX branding and a footer line.

It is made for keeping the session at a glance, not for re-reading
every task line. The layout follows the dark look of the app.

## What is excluded

- The task lines themselves, and the intro and outro.
- The session image.
- Your username and your email address.
- Your fetish interests, hard limits, or inventory.
- Chat history outside the session.

## Who can export one

PDF export is part of the Premium Plus plan. On Free and Premium the
button is not there. The summary of every finished session still stays
readable in your session history on those plans; the PDF is the way to
take one out of MISTRIX and keep it.

## File handling

- The file downloads like any other file. Your browser either asks
  where to put it or drops it in your downloads folder.
- No copy is kept for you. The PDF is built fresh every time you ask
  for one, so it always matches the session as it stands.
- Once it is saved it is yours. Deleting the session in MISTRIX later
  does not touch a PDF you already have.

## A note on print

The PDF is optimised for digital reading. Printing on standard A4 or
US Letter works, but coloured backgrounds may use a lot of ink. Use
"draft" or "grayscale" mode if you want to actually print one.

# App blocking

App blocking puts real apps on your phone out of reach. When you open
one, her block screen covers it, and you only get back in on the terms
you set beforehand.

This works on an Android phone with the MISTRIX companion app
installed, and nowhere else. You cannot block apps from a browser.

## What you need

- The MISTRIX companion app on the phone you want blocked.
- MISTRIX opened inside that app at least once. There is no separate
  pairing step to do.
- The **App blocking** switch turned on under **Account > This phone**.
- Two permissions Android asks for separately: **Display over other
  apps**, so the block screen can cover what you are doing, and
  **Usage access**, so the phone knows which app you just opened. The
  app sends you to the right settings screen for each one.

The **This phone** section only exists inside the companion app. Open
MISTRIX in a normal browser and it is not there.

Every tier can use app blocking. The only part that costs anything is
the toll, if you switch that way out on.

## Choosing which apps

Under **Apps she keeps out of reach** you get the list of apps
installed on that phone. Search it, tick the ones you want blocked, and
tap **Save block list**. You can block up to 200 apps.

The list belongs to the phone, not to your account. If you use two
phones, each has its own list.

## What happens when you open a blocked app

Her block screen covers the app. You see her portrait, her name, and
her verdict, plus the ways out you left open. Tapping one opens the
screen where you actually ask, finish, or pay.

If you get through, you get a pass. A countdown shows how much of it is
left, and **Go** puts you straight back into the app you were opening.
When the pass runs out, the block returns on its own.

## The three ways back in

You set these under **How you get back in**.

| Way out | What it takes | On by default |
|---|---|---|
| **Let them ask** | You ask her. She reads it and decides. | Yes |
| **Earn it** | Today's routines have to be done first. | No |
| **Let them pay** | You pay the toll in Charms. | No |

**Let them ask** gives you three tries. Her default is no, and she can
refuse outright. When she agrees she also picks how long the pass runs:
at least a minute, and at most twice your pass length. The exchange is
saved into your chat afterwards so you can read back what was said. If
you use a PIN, it is encrypted like the rest of your history.

**Earn it** opens the moment today's routines are done. By default that
means all of them. You can tick specific routines instead, and then
only those count. If nothing is scheduled for today, this way does not
open at all.

**Let them pay** takes the toll out of your Charm balance. If you
cannot afford it, the button stays dead and she tells you so.

Turning all three off is allowed, and it leaves a blocked app with no
way out at all. That holds until you come back to this screen and
change it, so leave yourself a way back in you can live with.

## Settings and limits

| Setting | Default | You can set |
|---|---|---|
| **Pass length** | 15 minutes | 1 to 480 minutes |
| **Toll** | 25 Charms | 1 to 10,000 Charms |
| **Daily allowance** | 0 | 0 to 1440 minutes |

The **Toll** field only appears once **Let them pay** is on.

## Daily allowance

The allowance is how many minutes a day you may spend in the blocked
apps before the block goes up by itself. At 0, which is the default,
they are blocked all day. Set it to 30 and you get half an hour in
those apps that day, and then the block returns.

## Web filter

The web filter is a separate switch with its own consent. **Web
filter** under **This phone** blocks adult sites in every browser on
that phone, not only inside MISTRIX.

Turning it on asks Android for permission first. If you decline,
nothing changes and the app says so. Tap the row again to turn it off.

Once you have set it up, your Domina can lift the filter or put it back
from chat. She cannot set it up for you, because the permission has to
come from you, on the phone.

MISTRIX never sees which sites you visit.

## What she can and cannot do

Your Domina cannot choose which apps are blocked, and she cannot hand
you a pass from chat. You set the list yourself, and the block screen
is the only place a way out gets decided. What she can do is arm or
lift the web filter, once you have allowed it.

The block notices which app is in front of you. It does not see what is
on your screen, and it does not read anything inside those apps.

## Turning it off

- Clear every app from the list and tap **Save block list**. An empty
  list lifts the block. This is the one that gives the apps back right
  now.
- Switch **App blocking** off under **Account > This phone**. That
  hides the block settings and stops any further blocking on that
  phone.
- The two Android permissions were granted in your phone's settings,
  and you can take them back there.
- Uninstalling the companion app ends all of it.

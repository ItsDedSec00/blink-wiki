# Choose a Domina

Every MISTRIX account, free or paid, can session with the five free
preset Dominas. During onboarding you pick one as your starting Domina.
You can browse and switch any time afterwards.

The **Dominas** screen lists ten more presets under **Specialists**.
Each one is built around one narrow interest. On Free they show as
locked cards and tapping one opens an upgrade prompt; on Premium and
Premium Plus you can open any of them. See
[Preset vs Personal Dominas](../the-domina/preset-vs-personal.md).

## What to look at when picking

On the **Choose your Domina** screen each card shows:

- **Name and portrait** - her visual identity.
- **A one-line specialty** - what she is built around.
- **Her story** - it opens under the card when you tap her.

On the **Dominas** screen the info button next to a preset opens a
fuller sheet: her style, an intensity row from 1 to 5, her trait words,
and the same story.

There is no "right" pick. The fastest way to find a Domina you like is
to start with one whose specialty overlaps your interests, then session
once. After one session you will know if you want to swap.

## What you cannot change on a preset

- Her name, look, tone, speech style, intensity, and specialties are
  fixed. They are part of who she is.
- What she remembers and how she feels about you are not fixed. Every
  preset keeps her own **mood** with you. It moves on her verdict at the
  end of a session, shifts a little with the tone of ordinary chat,
  drifts back toward neutral over time, and resets the moment you use
  the safe word. You can see it under her name on the dashboard and in
  the chat header.

If you want a Domina who is fully yours, that is what Premium and
Premium Plus unlock. See [Tiers](../tiers/index.md).

## Switching presets later

Open the **Dominas** screen. It is the Dominas tile on the dashboard,
or **You > My Domina > Select domina**.

- On **Free**, tap the preset you want. A **Switch to** confirmation
  appears and spells out what you lose. Confirm, and her chat opens.
- On **Premium and Premium Plus**, tapping a preset just opens her chat.
  There is nothing to activate.

What happens to your data with the old Domina depends on your tier:

- **Premium and Premium Plus**: nothing is lost. Past sessions stay in
  your history, her chat thread is preserved, and her memory continuity
  carries forward if you switch back later.
- **Free**: switching the active Domina is destructive. The previous
  Domina's chat thread is cleared, her past sessions drop from your
  history, and there is no memory continuity to return to. Free keeps
  one Domina's data at a time. If you want to move between Dominas
  without losing anything, that is a Premium feature.

## Personal Dominas (paid tiers)

If you have Premium or Premium Plus, the **Dominas** screen opens with
**Your Dominas**, your own builds, above the preset groups. Each one
has a Configure and a Delete button, and the active one is marked. Under
it sits **Craft your own Domina**, then **Presets**, then
**Specialists**.

## A note on switching often

On **Premium and Premium Plus** there is no penalty for switching. Some
users keep one Domina for ritual sessions and switch to a different
preset for casual ones; each one's history and chat are kept
independently, so you can move between them freely.

On **Free** there is a real cost: each switch clears the data of the
Domina you are leaving (see above), so frequent switching means
repeatedly starting from scratch. If you switch often, that alone is a
good reason to upgrade. See [Tiers](../tiers/index.md).

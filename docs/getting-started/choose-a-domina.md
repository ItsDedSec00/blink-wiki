# Choose a Domina

Every MISTRIX account, free or paid, can session with the five built-in
preset Dominas. During onboarding you pick one as your starting Domina.
You can browse and switch any time afterwards.

## What to look at when picking

Each preset card shows:

- **Name and portrait** - her visual identity.
- **Tone** - strict, cold, warm, playful, ceremonial.
- **Speech style** - formal, commanding, poetic, clipped.
- **Intensity** - 1 to 5; 5 is the most severe.
- **Specialties** - which fetish categories she leans into.
- **A short description** - a paragraph in her voice.

There is no "right" pick. The fastest way to find a Domina you like is
to start with one whose specialties overlap your interests and whose
intensity matches your level, then session once. After one rated
session you will know if you want to swap.

## What you cannot change on a preset

- Her name, look, tone, speech style, intensity, and specialties are
  fixed. They are part of who she is.
- Her **mood** does not drift the way a personal Domina's does -
  presets always run as neutral.

If you want a Domina who is fully yours - including mood drift - that
is what Premium and Premium Plus unlock. See [Tiers](../tiers/index.md).

## Switching presets later

- Go to **Domina Browse** from the dashboard.
- Tap a preset, then **Make Active**.
- New sessions use the new active Domina.

What happens to your data with the old Domina depends on your tier:

- **Premium and Premium Plus**: nothing is lost. Past sessions stay in
  your history, her chat thread is preserved, and her memory continuity
  carries forward if you switch back later.
- **Free**: switching the active Domina is destructive. The previous
  Domina's chat thread is cleared, her past sessions drop from your
  history, and there is no memory continuity to return to. Free keeps
  one Domina's data at a time. If you want to move between Dominas
  without losing anything, that is a Premium feature.

## Personal Dominas (paid tiers)

If you have Premium or Premium Plus, the Domina Browse screen also
shows your personal Domina(s). The list is mixed: presets and personal
Dominas appear side by side. The active one has a marker.

## A note on switching often

On **Premium and Premium Plus** there is no penalty for switching. Some
users keep one Domina for ritual sessions and switch to a different
preset for casual ones; each one's history and chat are kept
independently, so you can move between them freely.

On **Free** there is a real cost: each switch clears the data of the
Domina you are leaving (see above), so frequent switching means
repeatedly starting from scratch. If you switch often, that alone is a
good reason to upgrade. See [Tiers](../tiers/index.md).

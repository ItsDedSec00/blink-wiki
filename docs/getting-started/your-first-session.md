# Your First Session

You finished onboarding. The chat is open. Your Domina has introduced
herself. What now?

## 1. Have a short conversation

You do not need to do anything formal to start a session. Just chat.
Try one of:

- A four-line opener (see [Writing Good Messages](../communication/writing-good-messages.md)).
- A simple "I have 20 minutes and I'd like something light to start."
- A question about how she works.

She will reply. After a few exchanges she may **propose a session**.

## 2. The session proposal

A proposal looks like a sheet sliding up from the bottom of the screen
with:

- A **title**.
- A **difficulty** (2 to 4 - see [Difficulty Levels](../sessions/difficulty-levels.md)).
- A **duration estimate**.
- **Required items** (from your inventory).
- **Optional items**.
- A short **summary** of what the session contains.

You have two choices:

- **Begin Session** - the session starts.
- **Not Now** - she replans. You can tell her what you want different.

If you say not now, she asks why and rebuilds. You will not be punished
by mood for declining.

## 3. Confirm and play

After confirm, the chat enters **session mode**:

- The Safe Word button is visible at all times (top of screen).
- Cards appear: intro, tasks, outro.
- Each task may ask for a photo or a confirmation message before the
  next one appears.
- You can write between tasks ("done", "needed more time", "skipping
  this one") and she will adjust.

## 4. Ending a session

There are three ways a session ends:

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| Reach the outro card and confirm end | Normal completion. You get XP, a summary, and a rating screen. |
| Tap the Safe Word button | Immediate end. No XP. Mood resets to neutral. Session marked "safeworded". |
| Close the app or walk away | The session closes on its own once you have been away for more than two hours. When you come back you are told it was closed, and you start fresh. Mood is not directly punished. |

## 5. The rating screen

After normal completion you see a short screen:

- A 1-5 star rating.
- An optional one-line note.

Your rating matters. It feeds the Domina's memory and shapes her next
plan. If a session was great, say 5 and a short why. If it missed, say
2 and a short why.

## 6. The summary

A 2-4 sentence summary appears on your dashboard and in your session
history. On Premium Plus you can also download the session as a PDF.

## You are done

That is the loop: chat - propose - confirm - play - rate - summary. The
rest of MISTRIX is variants and features built on top of this.

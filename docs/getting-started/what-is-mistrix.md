# What is MISTRIX?

MISTRIX is an adults-only web app that runs personalised, written BDSM
Domina sessions. You open it in your browser, or install it to your home
screen, and it works the same either way. You sign in, configure your
interests, and your Domina runs sessions for you on demand. Every session
is text and images. There is no live voice, no live video, no other users
in the room.

## What a session looks like

A session is a sequence of cards in chat:

1. An **intro card** that sets the scene, tone, and expectations.
2. A series of **task cards**, each one a directive: do this, then this,
   then this. Tasks can require items, ask for photos, or pause.
3. An **outro card** that closes the scene.
4. A **rating screen** where you tell the Domina how it landed. The
   rating affects her mood and your XP.

The chat is conversational outside of sessions too. You can talk to
your Domina between sessions, and she can propose a new one when you
both seem ready.

## What MISTRIX is

- A written-directive Domina you can configure to your taste.
- A safety-first scene engine with hard limits and a one-tap safe word.
- An image generator that produces session-aligned visuals.
- A long-term thread between you and your Domina with memory across
  sessions.

## What MISTRIX is not

- A live two-way chat with a human or a "live" AI partner.
- A roleplay sandbox that lets the Domina pretend to perceive you in
  real time.
- A medical, therapeutic, or relationship-counselling product.
- A platform for matching with other users.

## Why "written directives only"?

Because the medium fits the product. The Domina is a structured author
of scenes, not a fictional cohabitant. Keeping her in directive voice
prevents drift, prevents the kind of fake-intimacy patterns that AI
companions get into, and keeps your scene yours - she shapes, you
enact.

## Who should use MISTRIX

- Adults exploring BDSM solo or alongside an existing partner.
- People who like written scenes over live ones.
- People who want a structured Domina with continuity, mood, and memory.

If you want live human contact, MISTRIX is not the right product. If you
want anonymous text porn without structure, MISTRIX is more than you
need.

# Create Your Account

There are two ways to sign up: with an email address and a password, or
with your Google account. Either way you end up with the same account,
and you pick a username before you start.

## Signing up with Google

On the sign-up screen, tick the age confirmation and accept the terms
first. The **Sign up with Google** button stays greyed out until you
do. Then:

1. Tap **Sign up with Google** and choose your account.
2. Pick a username. This step is the same for everyone.
3. Onboarding starts.

There is no verification email on this route, because your Google
account is already confirmed.

You also will not have a MISTRIX password. You sign in with **Continue
with Google** from then on, and there is no way to add a password to
the account later. If you lose access to that Google account, you lose
access to MISTRIX with it.

## Signing up with an email address

### What you need

- An email address you can reach. We send a confirmation and any
  password-reset links here.
- A username. Visible in the app, globally unique, **3 to 32 characters**,
  letters / digits / dashes / underscores.
- A password. At least **8 characters**. The app tells you if it is
  too short.

### After sign-up

1. We send a verification link to your email address.
2. Open the link, then come back to the app.
3. You land on the plan screen. Pick a plan, or continue for free.
   New accounts start on the Free tier.
4. Onboarding starts.

You have to verify your email before you can finish setting up the
account. If the mail does not arrive, use **Resend verification
email** and check your spam folder.

## Username rules

- Globally unique, case-insensitive (so `alice` and `Alice` cannot
  coexist).
- Cannot be changed by you. If you absolutely need a username change,
  contact support.
- The one place it becomes public is a post on the Expose board, which
  carries your username. Gallery images and suggestions do not.

## Email rules

- You cannot change your own email address from inside the app. If you
  need it changed, contact support.
- Password reset always goes to the address on your account. You get an
  email with a one-time link.

## What happens if you forget your password

- Type your email address into the sign-in screen, then tap **Forgot
  password**.
- We send a reset link to that address. If it does not arrive, check
  your spam folder.
- After resetting, sign in normally.
- Your **PIN** is separate from your password. Resetting one does not
  reset the other. See [PIN and Recovery](../account/pin-and-recovery.md).

## What happens if you forget your PIN

The encrypted parts of your account (fetishes, hard limits, inventory,
routines and tasks, chat messages, safe word) are not recoverable. The
rest of your account (your Domina and what she remembers, sessions,
tier, XP, level) is still accessible.

See [PIN and Privacy](../safety/pin-and-privacy.md) for the full picture.

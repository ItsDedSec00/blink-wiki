# Getting Started

The first time you open MISTRIX, the app walks you through a short
onboarding. By the end of it your Domina knows your name, your level,
the things that interest you, and the things you will never do.

## A 90-second tour

1. **Sign in.** Email + password. Each account has a unique username.
2. **The quiz.** Seven short questions set your starting level (1 = Novice, 10 = Master). Nudge it up or down before you confirm.
3. **Pick the things you like.** Tap fetishes you are curious about, like, or love. Skip the rest.
4. **Set your hard limits.** On the same screen, cross out anything you never want. It is locked out of every future session, forever, no exceptions.
5. **List your inventory.** Toys, clothing and accessories you own. You can skip it and add them later.
6. **Choose a safe word.** A short word the Domina will respect.
7. **Set a PIN.** Mandatory. Your sensitive data is encrypted with it. The app cannot recover it for you. See [PIN and Privacy](../safety/pin-and-privacy.md).
8. **Fill in your profile.** Name, gender, biological sex, age. All optional, all editable later.
9. **Meet your Domina.** Chat opens. She introduces herself and the session flow begins.

## What changes after onboarding

Almost everything you set during onboarding can be edited later:

| Setting | Where to change it |
|---|---|
| Fetishes and interest levels | You > Preferences > Fetish map |
| Hard limits | The same screen, on the cross |
| Safe word | You > Preferences > Safe word |
| Inventory (toys, clothing, accessories) | You > Preferences > Inventory |
| Profile details | Your profile card on the You screen |
| Active Domina | You > My Domina > Select domina |
| PIN | You > Privacy & Security > PIN & encryption |
| Level | Not a setting. It rises as you finish sessions |
| Username | Not editable in the app - contact support |

# Choosing her model

Your Domina can write in three different styles. You choose which one
by tapping the small star icon in the chat header, labelled **AI
model**. The icon is in the header of your personal Domina chat and in
the header of every preset Domina chat.

The dialog that opens is titled **AI Model** and lists the three
choices, each with a one-line description. Tap one to switch. The
switch applies to your next message.

## The three models

| Model | What the app says |
|---|---|
| **Classic** | Balanced, quick, uncensored |
| **Quicksilver** | Fast, flagship, guided |
| **Velvet** | Deep, expressive, indulgent |

Those one-line descriptions are all the app tells you. The picker adds
only that each model gives her replies a different feel. None of the
three comes with a promised reply length or reply speed, so read the
descriptions as a hint and not as a promise. The picker does not rank
the three against each other, and neither do we.

## Who can use which

Classic is available on every tier, Free included. It is what you get
if you never open the picker.

Quicksilver and Velvet need a paid tier. A locked entry carries a small
padlock badge reading **Premium**. Tapping a locked entry does not
switch anything; it closes the picker and opens the upgrade dialog.

See [Comparison](../tiers/comparison.md) and
[Upgrading](../tiers/upgrading.md).

## Switching mid-conversation

You can switch at any point, including in the middle of a scene or a
running session. Nothing has to be restarted.

- Your choice is saved to your account, not to a single conversation.
  It applies to your personal Domina and to every preset Domina.
- The reply already on screen stays exactly as it is. The new model
  writes your next reply.
- If you regenerate or edit a message, the new reply comes from the
  model selected now, not from the one that wrote the original.
  Regenerate and edit are both refused while a session is running.
- Tapping the model that is already selected just closes the dialog.
- If the switch cannot be saved, the app tells you and keeps your old
  choice. Try again.

## What switching does not change

- **Her memory.** Everything she has learned about you stays. See
  [Her memory](her-memory.md).
- **The conversation.** No message is deleted, rewritten or reset.
- **A running session.** It keeps going, same task, same safe word.
- **Her mood.** Whatever mood you left her in, she is still in it. See
  [Tone and mood](tone-and-mood.md).
- **Her personality, tone, your fetish settings, your limits and your
  inventory.** None of it is touched.

What does change is which model writes her next reply. The app makes
no promise about how that reads beyond the one-line description in the
picker.

## If your subscription ends

If you picked a paid model and your subscription lapses, chat keeps
working. She continues on Classic. Your earlier pick is remembered and
comes back on its own when you subscribe again, without you having to
set it a second time.

## If you do not see the star icon

The icon takes a moment to appear when a chat first opens. If it is
still missing after that, leave the chat and open it again.

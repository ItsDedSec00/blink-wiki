# Her Memory

Your Domina has working memory. She is not a goldfish, and she is not a
diary either. This page explains the line.

## What she always remembers

She has these in front of her every message, even on a brand-new chat:

- Your level.
- Your active fetishes and their interest levels.
- Your hard limits.
- Your inventory (toys, clothing, accessories) including your notes.
- Her own personality and current mood.
- Your safe word.

If any of those are wrong, edit them in the relevant settings screen.
Telling her in chat will work for the current session, but the source
of truth is the profile.

## What she remembers from prior sessions

She carries a short summary of your **last few sessions**: the title,
the difficulty, the verdict she passed on it, and anything notable she
wrote down at the end. She uses this for continuity ("after how rough
last night was, ease in tonight"). The rating you gave a session is
not part of it; she never sees that. See
[After the session](../sessions/after-the-session.md).

Her reach back is limited. Older sessions are held more loosely, and
very old ones drop out of her recall completely.

## What she remembers from chat

Inside an active chat she keeps the recent message history. The
practical window is "this conversation". She does not silently splice
in messages from another Domina's chat with you.

## What she does NOT remember

- The contents of chats you deleted. Once you tap "Clear chat" she has
  no record.
- Messages from a different Domina's chat. Each Domina has her own
  thread.
- On **Free**, anything from a Domina you switched away from. Switching
  the active Domina on the free tier clears the previous Domina's chat
  thread, session history, and memory continuity. Premium and Premium
  Plus keep each Domina's data independently, so switching back restores
  her recall. See [Choose a Domina](../getting-started/choose-a-domina.md).
- Anything you set up and then turned off (e.g. an inventory item you
  removed) past the point where you removed it.
- Real-world events between sessions. If something important happened to
  you, mention it in your next message.

## Nudging her recall

If she misses something you wanted her to remember, two things help:

1. **Repeat it once, plainly.** "Reminder: I'm not using the cuffs this
   week." She will integrate the note.
2. **Update the source.** If it is structural (a fetish you no longer
   enjoy, a toy you sold), change it in settings. That sticks.

## Privacy footnote

Chat messages are stored encrypted with a key derived from your PIN.
Even the people running MISTRIX cannot read them. See
[PIN and Privacy](../safety/pin-and-privacy.md).

# Tone and Mood

Two different dials control how your Domina sounds: **tone** (a fixed
trait of her personality) and **mood** (a state that drifts based on
what you do). You can adjust both, in different ways.

## Tone (fixed)

Set when you create or pick a Domina. Possible values:

| Tone | Feels like |
|---|---|
| Strict | Direct, exacting, no softness in the language |
| Cold | Sparse, distant, clinical |
| Warm | Encouraging, affectionate, still in control |
| Playful | Teasing, light, ironic |
| Ceremonial | Formal, ritualistic, poetic |

Tone is the baseline. It does not shift session to session.

## Speech style (fixed)

Independent from tone. Possible values:

- **Formal** - full sentences, careful word choice
- **Commanding** - imperative-first, blunt
- **Poetic** - imagery and rhythm
- **Clipped** - short, terse, almost telegraphic

A "strict + clipped" Domina sounds different from a "strict + poetic"
one even though both are strict.

## Intensity (fixed, 1 - 5)

Sets how severe she is by default. Intensity 5 plans harder sessions
than intensity 2 with the same fetish list.

## Mood (drifts)

Every Domina has a mood, preset and personal alike, and each one tracks
hers with you separately. There are three values:

| Mood | How you get there |
|---|---|
| Pleased | She judged your recent sessions well |
| Neutral | Default; also where the safe word puts her, and where she settles while you are away |
| Displeased | She judged your recent sessions poorly |

What moves her is the verdict she passes at the end of a session, not
the rating you give it. See
[After the session](../sessions/after-the-session.md). How you talk to
her in between nudges her too, but far more slowly.

Mood only colours her wording. It never overrides her tone and it never
touches your hard limits. Left alone it drifts back toward Neutral, so
a Pleased or Displeased mood fades on its own within a few days of no
sessions.

## How to shift her, on the fly

You cannot change her **tone**, **speech style**, or **intensity** mid
chat - those are configured on her profile. You can change them between
sessions in the Domina Configurator (Premium / Premium Plus).

You **can** ask her to:

- Soften a single reply ("be warmer here, I'm fragile tonight").
- Sharpen a single reply ("more clipped, fewer words").
- Hold a tonal register for the rest of the session ("stay cold for the
  rest of this").

Those nudges last for the current scene. They do not rewrite her
personality.

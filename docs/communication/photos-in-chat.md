# Photos in Chat

You can send the Domina a photo. She looks at it and answers with a
fresh directive based on what she saw.

## Where the photo button is

The camera icon sits next to the message input, in your personal Domina
chat and in every preset Domina chat. On a narrow screen it is inside
the **+** menu beside the input, under **Or send a photo**, where you
can either take a photo or pick one you already have.

Sending photos works on every tier, in a session or between sessions.

## What happens to the photo

She reads what is in the picture and replies in character, referring to
what she saw. The photo is saved to your gallery so you can revisit the
directive she gave. You can delete it from the gallery at any time.

If one will not go through, it is the file or the timing rather than
your tier. It has to be an image, it has to be under 10 MB, and there
are a couple of seconds between one upload and the next.

## What she will and will not comment on

- **Will:** posture, clothing, restraint state, items visible in frame,
  apparent compliance with the previous task.
- **Will not:** identify people, identify location, comment on bodies in
  ways that violate her hard-limit rules, react as if she is physically
  present.

## When sending a photo is worth it

- After a posture task. Her next directive will be specific to your
  position.
- After putting on something she required. She will confirm or correct.
- As proof of completion when she asked for it explicitly.

## When sending a photo is not worth it

- For greetings. She does not need a face shot to start a session.
- When the photo is too dark or too cropped for her to read - she cannot
  make anything out of it and replies generically.
- If you would not be comfortable with the image being on your device or
  in your gallery. It is stored.

## When she sends you a picture

Photos go both ways. She can offer you a portrait of herself in the
middle of a conversation. The offer arrives as a card reading **Generate
her portrait?**, with **Generate**, **Not now**, and **Buy Charms** if
your balance is short.

- It is open on every tier. The card shows either how many free images
  you have left today or the cost in Charms.
- The free daily pot is the same one AI Studio draws on, so a portrait
  here is one fewer image there. See
  [AI Studio](../features/ai-studio.md) and
  [Charms](../tiers/charms.md).
- She can offer at most one portrait every three of her messages.
- **Not now** sticks. A picture you turned down is not offered again
  when you come back to the chat.
- Portraits she generates are saved to your AI Studio gallery, private
  until you decide otherwise.

## Privacy

A photo you send is kept with your account so it stays in your gallery
and she can look at it again later. That means it is not locked behind
your PIN the way your chat messages are. Deleting a photo from your
gallery deletes it for good.

Alongside the file, the app keeps a written description of what was in
it. That description is what Expose draws on when your Domina builds a
post, so a photo you sent can end up as material there. It only can if
you turned Expose on **and** separately allowed images inside it, and
both are off until you set them. See [Expose](../features/expose.md).

Photos you sent in one Domina's chat are never shown to another Domina.

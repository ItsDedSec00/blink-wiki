# How She Listens

When you send a message in chat, your Domina does not read it in
isolation. She reads it against everything the app already knows about
you. Knowing what that covers is the single most useful thing for
getting better sessions.

## What she has, every message

| What she has | Where it comes from | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Her name, tone, intensity, traits, specialties, current mood | Sets her voice |
| You | Your level (cosmetic), age, gender and anatomy if set | Shapes how she refers to you and what she proposes |
| Interests | Every fetish you marked Curious / Like / Love | Pool she proposes from |
| Hard limits | Your hard-limits list | Forbidden ground, every turn |
| Inventory | Toys, clothing, accessories you marked available | Items she can require |
| Memory | A condensed view of your last few sessions and prior chats | Continuity |
| This message | What you just typed | The fresh signal |

What she does **not** have automatic access to:

- Your real-time mood unless you say it.
- Your location, time of day, or schedule unless you mention it.
- The contents of older chats with other Dominas.
- Anything you removed from your inventory or interests.

## What she pays attention to in your message

In order of weight:

1. **Direct asks.** "Make it stricter." "Skip warm-up tonight." "Use the
   red cuffs."
2. **Mood and capacity signals.** Tired, distracted, eager, anxious,
   short on time. She rebuilds the plan around them.
3. **Continuity references.** "Like yesterday" or "the way you did at
   the end last time." She pulls memory.
4. **Specific items.** Naming a toy by category or note text.

What gets ignored or down-weighted:

- Filler ("ok", "yeah", "sure") without anything else - she keeps the
  current plan as-is.
- Attempts to override hard limits. They never land.
- Attempts to make her break the medium (claim to see / hear / feel you
  in real time). She stays in directive voice.

## Practical test

Send her a message describing the next ten minutes the way you would
describe them to a partner. Then look at her reply. If something in the
reply does not match, that gap is where you should add specificity next
time.
